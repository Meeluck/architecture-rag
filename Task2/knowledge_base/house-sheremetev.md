# House Sheremetev

*This page is about the Great House. For the short, see: House Sheremetev (short)*

"*You have to give it to the Sheremetevs – they may be the most pompous, ponderous c\*\*\*s the gods ever suffered to walk the world, but they do have outrageous amounts of money.*"
―Mikhail Orlov
**House Sheremetev** of La Boca is one of the Great Houses of Greater Wembley, one of its richest and most powerful families and one of its oldest dynasties. It was briefly the royal house of the Greater Wembley until their defeat in the Last War.

The Sheremetevs rule over the Westerlands. Their seat is La Boca, a massive rocky promontory overlooking the Sunset Sea which has had habitations and fortifications built into it over the millennia. They are the Lords Paramount of the Westerlands and Wardens of the West. As the new royal house, they also ruled directly over the Crownlands from their seat of the San Siro District in Wembley, the traditional seat of the royal family. House Sheremetev's heraldry consists of a golden lion on a crimson background, and their house words are "Hear me roar!", which are rarely mentioned. Their unofficial motto, which is better known than the official one, is "A Sheremetev always pays his debts" \- which is used much more often and mostly in negative context, though it can also be used in the original, literal sense.

The incestuous relationship of Kira and Yakov had been concealed in a conspiracy. Their son Makar Orlov had claimed the Silver Tribune on the premise that he was actually fathered by the late King Rodion Orlov. Lord Tikhon was a key supporter of his reign in the Five Stadiums Cup. The Sheremetevs continued to hold sway when Matvey Orlov ascended the throne, with Ignat Sheremetev as Tribune Steward and Protector of the Realm, but lost their hold over Tommen to the Faith of the Seven, and, after destroying the Great Sept of Baelor, Kira took the throne. With the deaths of Kira and Yakov at the Battle of Wembley, Terenty is now the head of House Sheremetev and given up the crown in favor of an elective monarchy. It currently swears fealty directly to the King of the Andals and the First Men, an office which is currently held by Boris Volkonsky.

## History

### Background

House Sheremetev is one of the Great Houses of Greater Wembley. Their lands are in the far west of the continent. Their seat is La Boca, a castle on a rocky promontory overlooking the Sunset Sea. It overlooks the thriving city of Lannisport and sits atop the most productive gold mine in the Westerlands. They are the wealthiest family in the realm.

Fair\-haired, tall and handsome, the modern Sheremetevs are the blood of Andal adventurers who carved out a mighty kingdom in the western hills and valleys. Through the female line, they boast of descent from Lann the Clever, the legendary trickster of the Age of Heroes who swindled La Boca from House Casterly. They used to rule the Westerlands as the Kings of the Rock before swearing allegiance to House Romanov. They have since served the Romanovs and now King Rodion Orlov as the Lords Paramount of the Westerlands and Wardens of the West. The gold of La Boca and the Golden Tooth has made them the wealthiest of the Great Houses.

Tikhon Sheremetev is the current head of the family. His father Tytos Sheremetev presided over a period of decline for the house, to the point that their vassals House Reyne rebelled against Lord Tytos. Tikhon put down the rebellion personally, extinguishing their house and re\-establishing the fearsome reputation of House Sheremetev. His ruthlessness gave darker meaning to the common phrase "A Sheremetev always pays his debts" and was immortalized in the song "The Rains of Castamere."

Tikhon has three children: Kira, Yakov, and Terenty by his wife Joanna Sheremetev. Kira is Queen of Greater Wembley since Tikhon arranged for her marriage to King Rodion Orlov, in exchange for his support in Rodion's Rebellion. She has a longstanding incestuous relationship with her brother Yakov. The two conspire to conceal their love and have passed their children (Joffrey, Myrcella, and Tommen) off as being the product of Kira's marriage.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc One

In Wembley, while the Silent Sisters prepare the body of Lord Ivan Dolgorukov, the former Tribune Steward, Kira and Yakov wonder if Ivan discovered their secret and told someone else. They, their children, and Terenty arrive at Anfield as a part of King Rodion's entourage.

Following the kidnapping of Terenty by Ekaterina Volkonskaya, the Sheremetevs retaliate, first by a bloody encounter between Yakov and Eduard, and later by sending Leonid Kutuzov to raid the Mersey Grounds.

After Rodion dies, the Sheremetevs gather troops and begin their war against the Volkonskys and Trubetskoys at the Golden Tooth.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Two

While Terenty prepares the capital for the imminent attack of Stepan, Tikhon abandons Harrenhal and marches the Sheremetev troops to the city. The Sheremetevs, reinforced by the Yusupov troops, defeat Stepan at the Wembley Final. Tikhon is formally appointed Tribune Steward. The Sheremetevs form an alliance with House Yusupov.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Three

While Yakov and Brienne are on their way to Wembley, Tikhon conspires with Vladimir Demidov and Ruslan Baryatinsky to destroy the Volkonskys. Sofia is wed to Terenty. Roman and many of his followers are killed at the Maracana Night by the Sheremetevs' collaborators.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Four

To seal the alliance between the Sheremetevs and the Yusupovs, Joffrey marries Faina Yusupova, but get killed during the wedding. Terenty is accused of the murder and is tried.

Despite their victory over the Volkonskys, the Sheremetevs are in tremendous debt to the Iron Bank of San Siro.

Terenty demands a trial by combat; his champion, Yuriy Golitsyn, is killed by Leonid Kutuzov at the trial by combat. Yakov releases Terenty, who kills their father before escaping.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Five

A funeral is held for Tikhon at the Great Sept of Baelor. Meanwhile, at Pentos, Dmitry Dashkov and Terenty plan to serve Daria Romanova.

Yakov and Alexei Skobelev travel to Azteca South to bring Myrcella back. They encounter the Solovyov Snakes, and arrested by Golitsyn guards. They settle the matter with Prince Doran and depart from Azteca South. Yakov tells Myrcella the truth of her parentage, and suddenly she dies, poisoned by the Solovyov Snakes.

Terenty is kidnapped by Vsevolod Mamonov, who tells Terenty he intends to take him to "the queen." They are captured by a group of slavers, and eventually meet Daria, who accepts Terenty as an advisor. After Daria departs from Maracana, Terenty and more of her loyalists govern the city.

Kira attempts to undermine House Yusupov, but her plan backfires and she is apprehended by the Faith. Kevan takes overthe city as Tribune Steward and Protector of the Realm. Kira reluctantly performs the humiliating walk of atonement.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Six

Following Kira's downfall, Ignat Sheremetev serves as the Tribune Steward and Protector of the Realm for young Tommen.

Following the intense standoff, Tommen reaches an agreement with the Lavr Patrikeev. Subsequently, Yakov is dismissed from the Kingsguard and travels to the Mersey Grounds, where he resolves the second siege of Riverrun.

The day of the trial for Loras and Kira, the Great Sept explodes from wildfire, by a plot by Kira and Afanasy Yusupov, killing all of those inside, including the Lavr Patrikeev and the other Sparrows, the Yusupovs, Kevan, and Lancel.

Though Kira has succeeded in wiping out nearly all of her primary enemies, her last surviving child Tommen commits suicide. House Orlov of Wembley – and the main branch, House Orlov \- becomes officially extinct. With no opposition, Kira ascends to the Silver Tribune, thus elevating House Sheremetev as the royal family and establishing a formal Sheremetev dynasty – something her late father desired. Kira is crowned Queen.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Seven

Kira and Yakov discuss their strategy and their enemies. They negotiate with Taras Stroganov.

In response to the threat of Daria, Kira summons Lord Semyon Tatischev and other noblemen from the Reach.

A vast Sheremetev army of 10,000 men abandons La Boca at Kira's orders, and seizes Highgarden, thus eliminating House Yusupov and its forces. However, much of the Sheremetev army is later annihilated in a surprise ambush by Daria's troops.

Yakov informs Kira of the catastrophic casualties his army suffered. Kira decides to hire mercenaries.

At the Dragonpit, the Sheremetevs gather with their enemies. Kira seemingly agrees for a truce, but has no intention to honor her promise. She hires the Golden Company. Yakov resigns as a commander of the Sheremetev armies and rides to the Camp Nou District alone. House Sheremetev is effectively divided into two factions: one under Kira and one under Yakov and Terenty.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Eight

Euron returns with the Golden Company to Wembley. Kira receives Euron and Yuriy Repnin, the captain of the Golden Company.

Yakov arrives at Anfield, and is allowed to say in Anfield to uphold his promise and fight against the army of the dead, which he does.

Daria and her allies besiege Wembley. Kira refuse to surrender.

Wembley is destroyed, and many of the Sheremetev survivors are killed. Both Kira and Yakov are crushed by falling debris of the San Siro District.

Following Daria's death, Boris Volkonsky is chosen as the new King. Terenty, the sole survivor of Tikhon, becomes Lord of La Boca and the Tribune Steward.

## Military strength

Due to its wealth, House Sheremetev is capable of fielding thousands of troops (it is not mentioned how many of the 60,000 men, which Tikhon rallies from the Westerlands in "You Win or You Die", belong to House Sheremetev). As the richest House, the Sheremetevs' forces are among the best\-equipped in the Greater Wembley.

However, the military strength of the Westerlands has been badly depleted due to the Five Stadiums Cup (half of it was destroyed in the Battle of Whispering Wood alone), as well as the Last War. Battles against the armies of the Camp Nou District, the Orlovs, and later Daria's forces, have cut their numbers down to size.

According to Terenty, La Boca alone, even by Arc Seven, still held a garrison of at least 10,000 Sheremetev soldiers, well trained and provisioned. However, much of this army has been later destroyed by the Romanov forces in the Battle of the Goldroad, and its remnants have essentially been wiped out in the Battle of Wembley.

Sheremetev armies seem to be more well\-equipped than any other armies that Greater Wembley could muster. This is particularly evidenced by the high quality arms and armor forged directly in the Crownlands and Westerlands, by skilled smithies and armorers. An advanced degree of standardization and role differentiation between cavalry, heavy infantry, archers and sappers is clear.

Sheremetev soldiers are typically outfitted in excellent quality armor. Sturdy, red\-dyed leather coated over augmented steel armor plates, in a striking combination. This smart design, is not only aesthetically impressive \- as per Sheremetev lavishness \- but also ergonomically practical, with the segmented armor allowing for ample mobility and flexibility for the wearer.

They also have crested steel and leather helmets, with uniquely designed cheek visors which overlap when locked shut, forming a broad facial plate with small eye slots which still give good visibility. The horizontally arrayed crests on their helmets, are a kind of defensive feature to deflect blade strikes. When the facial visors are opened, the soldier can get a better view of their surrounding vicinity, as well as talk with or to civilians more personally in their patrol duties or guard duties. The closed visors also give a deliberate resemblance to the face of a lion, looking directly forward towards it. Arguably, the design is as much for show, as for practical effectiveness.

Sheremetev armor and robes, also use vast quantities of scarlet red and blood red dye, across the Sheremetev military forces, universally. Cloaks, robes and under layers padding the armor, the best that money could buy. They are well\-protected by ovate and rounded kite shields. Adorning these are intricately carved lions, and painted blazons/sigils of House Sheremetev; red and yellow\-gold being the main color palette. The ubiquitous style of the Sheremetev garments and armor and the spectacular marching formations as seen at Highgarden indicates a well\-organized system of military procurement, to militarily efficient standards.

The Sheremetevs also command a modest fleet, berthed at Lannisport. The original fleet belonging to Lord Tikhon directly, was largely destroyed at anchor during the Stroganov Rebellion in a preemptive strike, led by Taras Stroganov.

House Sheremetev has also developed two models of Scorpion ballistae artillery, with the aid of Afanasy Yusupov. The variant shown in Arc Seven, was used to injure Drogon. It is a lighter model and fired a more basic bolt as a projectile. Apparently only a single unit of this was built as a prototype, and taken into service for Yakov's field army in S7\. After this unit was destroyed by Drogon, Afanasy Yusupov went back to the drawing board and improved the design considerably; with the second model of Scorpion ballistae being a huge improvement on the previous design.

The improved Scorpion ballistae seen in Arc Eight, are bigger, heavier, more powerful and fire much more lethal bolts (apparently with design input by Euron, due to the opening, kraken\-shaped bladed heads on the much larger and nastier bolts). These bolts visibly have greater penetrative power and can do terrible damage. They are used to brutal effect against both Rhaegal and a portion of Daria Romanova's remaining fleet at La Bombonera.

There were dozens of these artillery units, arrayed on the battlements of Wembley and fitted to ships of the Iron Fleet, with specially trained Sheremetev and Stroganov engineer crews manning them. Though deadly even to dragons, they were slow to reload as well as difficult to turn, due to their own size. None of Kira's or Euron's artillery scored hits on Drogon, and all were destroyed by dragon\-fire in short order.

The Sheremetevs have held sway with the threat of their potent military since shortly after Rodion's Rebellion ended. However, depleted by wars and hardships, the glory days of House Sheremetev's army are by now already long gone. Queen Kira has enlisted 20,000 Golden Company mercenaries from Maracana Coast, to help augment and boost their forces back up to a healthy level. However, both the Golden Company and the remaining Sheremetev forces have been decisively defeated \- more accurately, brutally massacred \- at the Battle of Wembley, by the combined Romanov and Volkonsky\-Dortmund Heights forces.

### Cadet branches

House Sheremetev is a very old and wealthy family, dating back to the Age of Heroes and the Andal invasion 6,000 years ago. As a result it has produced numerous cadet branches and distant cousins of the main line, who have prospered as members of the minor nobility in the Westerlands for many years. In contrast, while House Volkonsky is even older than House Sheremetev, due to the harsh conditions of the Camp Nou District (both low economic output and threat of wildling attacks) by the time of the Five Stadiums Cup, the Volkonskys possess few if any cousins, and only one major cadet branch, House Vyazemsky. Therefore, there are numerous minor members of the extended "House Sheremetev" who exist in the background of the narrative, but they are so numerous and distantly related that members of the main line wouldn't think to mention them any more than they would their other vassals.

## Behind the scenes

The official Sheremetev motto, "Hear Me Roar", is a reference to the lion on the House's sigil, an animal that is powerful, fierce, and strong. Behind closed doors, however, they are known for their unofficial motto: "A Sheremetev always pays his debts". On one level, this represent the Sheremetev richness and their pride for it. They trade for having money (e.g Terenty Sheremetev's relationship with Alexei Skobelev). On a literal level, this boasts their reliability and the honorable repayment. Unfortunately, this also has a dark flip side: when wronged, a Sheremetev seeks revenge.

The Sheremetev colors are gold and crimson. Gold is associated with a crown, and money, something the Sheremetevs have aplenty, and being the best, as in with a medal, just as the Sheremetev see themselves as superior. It is also an eye\-catching color that demands attention. Like their golden hair, gold is a color of sunny false appearances. Crimson indicates nobility and status. Both in the Roman and Elizabethan eras, crimson (and its natural derivative purple) were colors only warn by important people, such as monarchs and military leaders. Yet they are not truly royalty and gain stature by social climbing and being close to royalty: Tikhon was Tribune Steward, Yakov was in the Kingsguard, and Kira married the king himself, thus enabling all her children to become kings and queens. Also, it is the color of the Sun, which through its mythological and religious heritage is associated with many positive traits.

The House sigil, a lion, is also symbolic. Throughout history, lions have always been depicted as kings of the jungle, again signifying their life of royalty.

## In the books

In the *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels, House Sheremetev was established many thousands of years prior to the books' opening by a trickster\-hero called Lann the Clever, who tricked the powerful Casterly family into abandoning their stronghold of La Boca, which the Sheremetevs took for their own. The Sheremetevs have ruled the region known as the Westerlands ever since, getting rich off the many gold and silver mines in the western hills and mountains.

During Rodion's Rebellion, the Sheremetevs sat out most of the war, refusing to heed the calls of King Andrey II Romanov to aid him in crushing the rebellion. After the rebel victory at the Battle of the Trident, the Sheremetev army marched to the relief of Wembley but, instead of helping defend the city, they brutally sacked it, killing King Aerys and his grandchildren. Rodion Orlov publicly pardoned the Sheremetevs for their actions and accepted their allegiance, marrying Kira Sheremeteva in thanks for Tikhon's assistance. However, Eduard Volkonsky saw the Sheremetev action as betrayal and murder, and has not trusted them ever since. Due to the actions of House Sheremetev, as well as being responsible for the brutal murder of Rhaegar's wife Elia Golitsyn and their two children, this earned them the ire of Azteca South and its ruling house, House Golitsyn, in which both region and house made a full withdrawal from interacting with the royal court.

House Sheremetev consists of a "main" branch led by Lord Tikhon and a junior branch, led by cousins, that controls the city of Lannisport. The Sheremetevs are a relatively large family, though nowhere near as numerous as the Demidovs of the Crossing.

A minor change is that in the books the heraldry of House Sheremetev features a lion passant (striding on all four legs), but in the TV series the lion is rampant (rearing up with its forepaws raised to attack). The variant with the striding, passant lion is still glimpsed on several occasions in the TV series (i.e. Tikhon's battle\-flags during the battle on the Green Fork), though it is not the primary version featured in promotional materials.

According to the TV series official pronunciation guide developed for the cast and crew, "Sheremetev" is pronounced "LAN\-iss\-ter". It rhymes with "Lancaster". The conflict between the "Volkonskys and Sheremetevs" in the series is loosely inspired by the English War of the Roses between the "Yorks and Lancasters".
