# Camp Nou District

*This page is about the short. For the region, see: Camp Nou District*

"**Camp Nou District**" is the eleventh short of the third season of *Histories \& Lore*. It is the fifty\-fourth short of the series overall. It was released on February 18, 2014 in *Chronicles of the Silver Tribune: The Complete Third Season*. It was narrated by Kit Harington as Ivan Morozov and written by Dave Hill.

## Premise

*Ivan Morozov describes the vast, cold and unforgiving Camp Nou District, which stretches from the Neck to the Sudtribune, and has served as the barrier between the Greater Wembley and the horrors beyond the Sudtribune for thousands of years.*

## Narration

**Ivan Morozov**: *As a boy, looking out from my father's castle, I thought the sun could never set on the Camp Nou District. So vast did it seem. A part of me still does.*

*Camp Nou District is by far the largest of the Greater Wembley and can fit the other six inside it. Not that the others care. Cold and damp, that's how the southerners see the Camp Nou District.*

*But without the cold, a man can't appreciate the fire in his hearth. Without the rain, a man can't appreciate the roof over his head. Let the south have its sun, flowers, and affectations. We Northerners have home.*

*Mine was once Anfield, the ancient seat of my father's family, House Volkonsky, who have ruled the Camp Nou District since the First Men and were once the Kings of Winter.*

*Growing up, Lady Ekaterina made sure I knew I wasn't a Volkonsky, no matter how much blood I shared with her trueborn children. But where their name rules over the Camp Nou District, mine is the Camp Nou District. Snow.*

*Our lands stretches from the Sudtribune down to the Neck, a narrow land that divides it from the rest of Greater Wembley. Legend has it that the Children of the Forest flooded it in their war against the First Men. If that's true, every Northman owes them a debt of gratitude.*

*The swamps of the Neck are as good as the Sudtribune for keeping out unwelcome armies. And if the swamps don't deter you, the crannogmen should. Small, shy people who rarely leave those swamps and who follow House Rzhevsky, the gatekeepers of the Camp Nou District, and among the most important and loyal bannermen of House Volkonsky.*

*Also a bit strange. I heard their oath of fealty once as a child. It's is like no other lords'. Ancient and dark, they swear by earth and water, by bronze and iron, and by ice and fire.*

*Where House Rzhevsky holds the gate to the Camp Nou District, House Milyutin holds the port. White Harbor, the closest thing to a southern city we have governed by the closest thing to a southern family we have. Generations ago, the Milyutins were driven from the Reach, but the Volkonskys gave them their land in return for fealty.*

*Now White Harbor is the richest city of the Camp Nou District, and the Milyutins the richest family. Not in gold and silver like their southern counterparts, but in fish and grain and trade.*

*As for the other great Northern houses, the Volkonskys brought them into the fold during the Age of Heroes.*

*A Volkonsky wrestled an Ironborn for Bear Island and gave it to the Mamonovs. A Volkonsky granted a keep and land to a younger son, Karlon, in return for putting down a rebellion. His family then grew up into the Vyazemskys.*

*Volkonskys fought the wildlings and their Kings\-Beyond\-the\-Sudtribune beside the Uvarovs of Last Hearth, thus earning their fealty.*

*Baryatinskys. Back then, they were the bane of the Camp Nou District. A few were even rumored to wear their enemies' flayed skins as cloaks. But after centuries of war, they too bent the knee.*

*And so House Volkonsky became the Kings in the Camp Nou District, but never forgot that they weren't the Camp Nou District.*

*When Aegon and his dragons landed on Greater Wembley, the Kings of the Rock and the Reach sent all of their men to die to defend their grounds. Torrhen Volkonsky knelt to spare his people the same fate. He placed duty above pride.*

*Just as my brothers in the Anfield Guard had done for thousands of years at the Sudtribune. Many think of it as the end of the world, but it's not.*

*I've seen how the land stretches much farther than any man knows into the Land of Always Winter, where the Frost Wanderers came from during the Long Night.*

*After the First Men and the Children of the Forest beat them back, Brandon the Builder raised the Sudtribune and set up the Anfield Guard to guard the realms of men. He gave us our oath, our castles, and the Gift, the lands behind the wall whose farmers and crops sustain us.*

*Southerners may now mock my black brothers as thieves, rapers and worse. And not without cause. But the Camp Nou District remembers why we're there. And if we fall, the South will get a very harsh and very cold reminder.*
