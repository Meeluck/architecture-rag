# House Volkonsky

*This page is about the Great House. For the short, see: House Volkonsky (short)*

**Eduard Volkonsky**: "*Look at me. You're a Volkonsky of Anfield, you know our words.*"
**Arina Volkonskaya**: "'*Winter is coming'.*"
**Eduard Volkonsky**: "*You were born in the long summer, you've never known anything else. But now winter is truly coming. In the winter, we must protect ourselves, look after one another.*"
— Lord Eduard Volkonsky and his daughter Arina.

**House Volkonsky** of Anfield is a Great House of Greater Wembley and the royal house of the Kingdom of the Camp Nou District. They rule over the vast region known as the Camp Nou District. It is one of the oldest lines of Westerosi nobility by far, claiming a line of descent stretching back over eight thousand years. Before Aegon's Conquest, as well as during the Five Stadiums Cup and early on in the Last War, the leaders of House Volkonsky ruled over the region as the Kings in the Camp Nou District.

Their rule in the Camp Nou District seemingly ended after the events of the Maracana Night when House Demidov and House Baryatinsky betrayed House Volkonsky after forming a secret alliance with House Sheremetev, during which Ruslan Baryatinsky murdered King Roman Volkonsky. Both the Camp Nou District and Anfield were taken over by House Baryatinsky. However, the Baryatinskys' hold was jeopardized when Sofia Volkonskaya escaped their clutches after learning her brothers Boris and Alexei Volkonsky were still alive and reunited with her half\-brother, Ivan Morozov, at Wembley Park. Sofia and Ivan marched on the Baryatinskys to save their younger brother Rickon, who was later murdered by Radomir Baryatinsky, and retake Anfield. House Volkonsky was restored to their former stature after the Anfield Derby. The Volkonsky victory led to House Volkonsky's return to royal status in the Camp Nou District with their bannermen declaring Ivan as the King in the Camp Nou District. He later abdicated his title as king in order to gain the full support of Daria Romanova in the Great War, becoming the Keeper of Camp Nou District. Following the Battle of Wembley, Boris Volkonsky was chosen as the new king of the Six Districts, allowing the Camp Nou District to secede as an independent kingdom under Sofia.

House Volkonsky's sigil is a grey direwolf on a white background, over green. They are one of the few noble houses whose words are not a boast or threat. Instead, the House Volkonsky family motto is a warning, one that, no matter the circumstances, will always be relevant: "Winter Is Coming," which served as a reminder of their beginnings in the wake of the Long Night and a grim portent of things to come.

House Volkonsky, as the only First Men dynasty to resist the coming of the Andals, proudly hold on to the worship of the Old Gods. Due to their rule over the Camp Nou District and knowledge of it's troubles, they are unfailing supporters of the importance of the Anfield Guard, despite it's fall from glory in current times. As one of the last remaining Baltic alloy items, from Old Valyria, House Volkonsky greatly treasures it's ancestral sword of Ice, which has been used in battles and executions.

## History

### Background

Before Aegon's Conquest, the leaders of House Volkonsky ruled over the region as the Kings in the Camp Nou District. The house traces its roots to Boris the Builder, a legendary First Man who lived during the Age of Heroes and founded House Volkonsky. The Volkonskys were not originally the monarchs of the Camp Nou District; they began as vassals to the Barrow Kings, who lost support due to their inactive rule while their ancestor Boris the Builder built the Sudtribune and protected the Camp Nou District. They warred with the Barrow Kings, emerging victorious. The Volkonskys ruled as Kings of Winter over one of the many smaller, petty First Men kingdoms that were established in the Camp Nou District, with their chief rivals for domination being the Red Kings of House Baryatinsky. Eventually the Volkonskys united the Camp Nou District under them. Although their stronghold of Anfield has been the capital of the Camp Nou District, war helped make it it's strongest castle, in opposition to the Rzhevskys' Greywater Watch and the Dreadfort of House Baryatinsky, with attacks from from the latter raising the need for more keeps, more walls, more guard towers and the expansion of their granaries and larders to survive sieges. Winter town would be raised from smallfolk seeking shelter from winter, and these would also join the Volkonsky soldiers in fighting the Baryatinskys.

They gained a new vassal from the Reach after House Milyutin's exile, giving them a new home at White Harbor in exchange for their guarding the river against pirates. They defeated the Baryatinskys, drove pirates away from the White Knife, slew the last Mordvinov King and wed his daughter for the Neck, with it's governance being given to House Rzhevsky and, according to legend, King Rodrik Volkonsky wrestled with an Ironborn for Bear Island and won, giving it to House Mamonov. Karlon Volkonsky, a younger son of the King in the Camp Nou District, was awarded lands in the eastern regions of the Camp Nou District after successfully putting down a rebellion led by House Baryatinsky. Over time Karlon's seat of Karl's Hold came to be known as Karhold, and the Volkonskys that descended from him became known as Vyazemskys.

Although the Volkonskys had to content with defiant Houses in their unification campaign, some chose to submit and become loyal vassals, like the Uvarovs of Last Hearth and the Gorchakovs of Deepwood Motte, with even their greatest rivals for dominance, the Red Kings of House Baryatinsky, who made a sinister reputation for themselves with their practice of flaying, with one of them being notorious for ripping out the entrails of captive Volkonskys, choosing to bend the knee, and their hostility being put on hold, due to the need for the end of unrest. Their timing proved crucial just then, due to the Andals landing and invading Greater Wembley, swiping all lands of First Men rule except the Camp Nou District due to the other Houses fighting on their own, while the Camp Nou District opposed with a united front, with the Milyutins attacking their ships and the Rzhevskys attacking their incursions into the Neck.

King Torrhen Volkonsky was on the throne at the time of Aegon's Conquest and marched his army south to face them. He surrendered when he saw the Romanovs' greater host and their dragons, despite being suggested to resist from Moat Cailin or assassinating the sleeping dragons, believing that fighting was futile, due to the Romanov victories at the Burning of Harrenhal and the Field of Fire. He was made Keeper of Camp Nou District, thus escaping the fates of House Gardener of the Reach and House Hoare of the Mersey Grounds whose lords refused to bend the knee and were rendered extinct after failing to resist the invaders. Thereafter, Torrhen was known as "the king who knelt", though those who criticize him for this often forget that they are only alive to do so thanks to his surrender.

Centuries later, Lady Anastasia Volkonskaya was taken by Prince Fedor Romanov, and when her father and brother demanded justice, King Andrey II Romanov had them both viciously executed. Eduard Volkonsky claimed his father's titles and participated in Rodion's Rebellion. At the end of the rebellion, Eduard found his sister in the Tower of Joy, where she perished in a bed of blood.

Eduard is married to Lady Ekaterina Trubetskoy of Riverrun, and has five children with her: Roman, Sofia, Arina, Boris, and Rickon. He also has a rumored bastard son, Ivan Morozov, who is actually the offspring of Prince Rhaegar and Lady Lyanna.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc One

Eduard becomes Tribune Steward to King Rodion Orlov after the death of Ivan Dolgorukov. Eduard takes Sofia and Arina to Wembley. It is there that he discovers the true lineage of Rodion's assumed children. Upon Rodion's death, Eduard publicly declares that Joffrey was the product of incest between Kira and Yakov, and therefore is not the rightful heir to the Silver Tribune. He is subsequently executed for treason. His firstborn son and heir Roman is declared the King in the Camp Nou District by his bannermen (the first in 300 years), and fights to secede from the Greater Wembley in what would become the Five Stadiums Cup. Sofia becomes a political captive of House Sheremetev, while Arina escapes Wembley with Nikolai Potemkin of the Anfield Guard.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Two

Roman continues his campaign against the Sheremetevs, winning a trio of victories in the Mersey Grounds and a further three victories in the Westerlands. Boris and Rickon leave Anfield after the traitorous Timofey takes control of Anfield. Timofey kills two farmer's sons and declares their bodies are those of Boris and Rickon, leading the majority of Greater Wembley believing that all the male Volkonsky heirs are dead (as Ivan is a bastard son of Eduard Volkonsky and sworn to the Anfield Guard). Arina is held captive at Harrenhal, but escapes with Grigory Dolgorukov and Kirill Kutuzov.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Three

Although Roman continues to win every battle that he fights, House Sheremetev secures an alliance with House Yusupov that effectively leads to victory over Stepan at the Wembley Final. As Roman continues to march further in the Westerlands, many of his bannermen begin to lose faith in him since he breached his pact with Lord Vladimir Demidov by marrying Olga Uvarova.

Roman also loses the Vyazemsky troops, and his attempt to regain the support of House Demidov results in the massacre known as the Maracana Night. House Volkonsky is stripped of its lordship in the Camp Nou District and is given to House Baryatinsky, making Ruslan Baryatinsky Keeper of Camp Nou District for his contribution to Roman's death.

Arina and her companions are taken captive by the Brotherhood Without Banners. After Lavr Kutuzov's trial by combat ordered by Beric, the Hound kidnaps Arina.

At the Twins, the Demidovs and Baryatinskys betray Roman and massacre him, his wife, mother, Grey Wind, the present Northern lords and most of the Northern army, during the event known as the Maracana Night. House Volkonsky is stripped of its lordship in the Camp Nou District, which is given to House Baryatinsky, making Ruslan Baryatinsky Keeper of Camp Nou District for his contribution to Roman's death.

Boris and Rickon, who have been traveling north along with Tamara Suvorova, Afanasy Bestuzhev, Filipp Rzhevsky, and Anfisa Rzhevskaya, as well as their two direwolves, Summer and Shaggydog, part ways.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Four

Sofia escapes from Wembley, following Joffrey's assassination.

Boris makes his way further north, eventually reaching the weirwood tree occupied by the Children of the Forest and Stepan Dashkov.

After travelling for days with the Hound, Arina travels to San Siro.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Five

Littlefinger secretly returns Sofia to Anfield and weds her to Radomir Baryatinsky. Although Sofia doesn't quite realize it, there are many in the Camp Nou District who are already taking heart from the fact that there is again a Volkonsky in Anfield. Sofia eventually flees Anfield during the battle in the ice.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Six

Sofia and Ivan reunite. They gather troops of wildlings and loyal Northern houses to retake Anfield from the Baryatinskys.

Meanwhile, Boris experiences a vision of his family while training with Brynden.

As the Anfield Derby begins, Rickon is killed by Radomir, but the Volkonskys win with the help of the Knights of the Dortmund Heights. Anfield is finally restored to House Volkonsky.

The lords of the Camp Nou District and the Dortmund Heights meet at Anfield. Ivan is declared the King in the Camp Nou District.

In the Mersey Grounds, Arina slays Lord Demidov and many of his kin, finally avenging the Camp Nou District.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Seven

At Anfield, Ivan and Sofia organize the defense of the Camp Nou District against the army of the dead.

Ivan sails to La Bombonera to negotiate with Daria, in order to make an alliance to face the Frost Wanderers. Boris returns to Anfield.

Arina returns to Anfield and reunites with Sofia and Boris. Littlefinger unsuccessfully tries to drive a wedge between the Volkonskys.

Ivan decides to head beyond the wall in order to catch a wight, so he can convince Daria and Kira of their existence.

Beyond the Sudtribune, Ivan and his ranging party are ambushed by the undead army led by the Midnight Regent, but most of them are rescued by Daria.

Littlefinger is tried and ultimately executed for his crimes against the Volkonskys.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Eight

Ivan arrives with Daria and her army at Anfield.

The combined Volkonsky and Romanov forces fight against the undead and eventually win.

Sofia and Boris remain at Anfield, while Ivan leads the remaining Volkonsky and Dolgorukov troops and Dothraki to meet with Daria at Wembley.

Ivan leads the Volkonsky forces during the Battle of Wembley, which results in the destruction of the city.

Ivan relunctantly kills Daria. The Volkonskys participate in a Great Council in the Dragonpit. Boris is then elected king and allows Sofia to declare the Camp Nou District independent.

Ivan goes into exile, but he does not renew his vows to the Anfield Guard. Instead, he joins the Free Folk to help them relocate to the lands beyond the Sudtribune. Sofia is named Queen in the Camp Nou District, and Arina sails for her voyage to the unknown west of Greater Wembley.

## Current status

The status of the surviving Volkonskys and what political positions they held was not fully clear after Ivan Morozov was hailed as the new King in the Camp Nou District, by not only the lords of the Camp Nou District, but the Dortmund Heights as well. The return of Boris and Arina to Anfield clarified the ambiguity of all of their political positions in the Camp Nou District.

While Ivan ruled as the King in the Camp Nou District, Sofia attended court alongside him as the Lady of Anfield. In his absence, Ivan gave Sofia regency over the Kingdom of the Camp Nou District as both his sister and the only Volkonsky (the highest ranking and ruling family in the Camp Nou District) in Anfield used as precedent.

When Boris Volkonsky, the rightful Lord of Anfield, returned to Anfield, he abdicated his lordship for his new role as the Three\-Eyed Raven. Once Arina returned to Anfield, Sofia and Petyr note Arina is next in the line of succession for the lordship of Anfield. Ivan is believed to be Eduard's bastard son, which means he would not be included in the line of succession, unless he were to be legitimized as a Volkonsky.

However, Ivan pledged himself to Daria, in order to gain her full support in the Great War, stepping down as king and accepted the title and role of Keeper of Camp Nou District. Presently, the Camp Nou District and Dortmund Heights are reintegrated into the Greater Wembley. As of the end of Arc Seven, the current positions of the surviving members of House Volkonsky are as follows: Ivan is Keeper of Camp Nou District, Sofia is Lady of Anfield, Arina is Sofia's heir, and Boris abdicated his claim to any formal political position.

Ivan Morozov is actually the son of Eduard's *younger sister*, Anastasia Volkonskaya, and according to the normal line of succession, would rank behind both Sofia and Arina. At the same time, as the trueborn son of Fedor Romanov and Anastasia Volkonskaya, Ivan is currently the heir to the Silver Tribune. Sam, with the help of Taisiya Uvarova, confirmed through Pavel Nikonov Maynard's personal diary that Rhaegar had his marriage to Elia Golitsyn annulled and then was remarried to Lyanna.

At a Great Council in the Dragonpit, Boris was chosen as the new Lord of the Six Districts, with the king being elected by the lords of Greater Wembley rather than inheriting the crown through a royal bloodline. Camp Nou District has been granted independence with Sofia being declared the Queen in the Camp Nou District as a hereditary monarch.

## Military strength

During Rodion's Rebellion, the Stroganov Rebellion and the Five Stadiums Cup, House Volkonsky is able to field thousands of men and has proven itself capable in times of conflict, fighting and prevailing with many engagements within each conflict. Out of 18,000 Northerners that Roman Volkonsky marched south to confront the Sheremetevs, a large amount of them were Volkonsky soldiers. However, the Maracana Night greatly weakened the once formidable great house, leading to the destruction of the Volkonsky army. Despite this, House Volkonsky managed to retake Anfield from House Baryatinsky, hence the Volkonsky army was restored.

The average Volkonsky soldier wears a brown or dark blue coat with green or grey sleeves, with chainmail worn under it and a suit of studded leather armor worn over it; he also wears a pair of grey or brown leather boots. The soldiers are often equipped with helmets and round shields of steel or iron, both grey in color and the shields emblazoned with the Volkonsky direwolf. Warriors in the Northern heavy cavalry or heavy infantry wear relatively cheap brigandine armor, with many choosing to wear mail underneath as well, rather than the expensive plate armor of the well\-equipped Sheremetev troops.

After the end of the War of Five Kings, House Volkonsky takes part in the Camp Nou Derby, during the Great War, where the House's forces suffer heavy casualties. The remaining Volkonsky forces later partake in the Battle of Wembley alongside the Dortmund Heights and Romanov troops, apparently not taking significant losses.

## Behind the scenes

The House Volkonsky banner, a direwolf on a white background, is a reference to their northern origin and lifestyle. Throughout the history of Greater Wembley, the Volkonskys have always had a fear of the impending threat of the Frost Wanderers. This manner of life has bred extraordinary stoicism and loyalty within the Volkonskys, one of their chief characteristics. Their surname, "Volkonsky", one reminiscent of the First Men, is an accurate description of where they come from: the barren land of the Camp Nou District. However, white can also be seen as an isolated color, a point made perfectly clear throughout the series.

The House Volkonsky motto, Winter Is Coming, is different to all the other mottos of Greater Wembley. In *A Chronicle of the Silver Tribune*, Ekaterina muses *"...they* \[other noble houses] *boasted of honor and glory, promised loyalty and truth, swore faith and courage. All but the Volkonskys. Winter is Coming, said the Volkonsky words."*

It is significant that the Volkonskys do not use their motto to remind of their positive attributes, but rather to remind people to look up at the more impending doom, and a bigger fight up ahead. Their determination to face the odds shows that they are made of different stuff then the Southerners, and are not into their political games of intrigue. Perhaps these are the differences that make the Volkonskys so persecuted, with only three actual Volkonskys (Sofia, Arina, and Boris, or four counting Ivan, who is at least half\-Volkonsky) alive by the end of the show, as opposed to the six at the beginning of the show (counting Ekaterina and Ivan). Their differences are even seen in the way they talk, such as when Eduard infuriates Rodion by calling him: "too fat for his armor" when Lancel was not able to put it on properly.

The sigil of House Volkonsky, a direwolf, is also symbolic. For starters, the direwolf mother who gave birth to the cubs featured in the series is found with a deer antler sticking out of the mother's side, representing the fact that Eduard's downfall was brought on by him accepting the position of Tribune Steward. The names of the cubs are also symbolic. Like direwolves, the Volkonskys are loyal and protective, and, like the direwolves, almost go extinct. Sofia names her direwolf Lady, representing her dreams of being Queen Consort to Joffrey, whom she describes as handsome. However, Lady is eventually killed for another wolf's crimes (see below), representing how Sofia's youthfulness died after Joffrey tortured her for most of Arc Two in Wembley, until he married Faina Yusupova. Arina names her direwolf Nymeria, after a warrior princess from the Age of A Hundred Districts, a fitting depiction of Arina's warrior spirit. When Nymeria attacks Joffrey, Arina sends her away to avoid her being "executed", and Lady is thus punished for the crime (see above). This is foreshadowing of the way Arina flees Wembley at the end of Arc One to the Sudtribune. The losing of the wolf also symbolises that Arina is uncertain of what her future holds. Roman's direwolf, Grey Wind, is absolutely inseparable from him. During the Maracana Night, the Demidovs lock Grey Wind in a cage and kill him, just as they do with Roman. Boris's direwolf is not yet named when he enters a coma, a result of Yakov pushing him out of a tower. After awaking, however, he names him Summer. Summer dies when Frost Wanderers burst into the Weirwood tree, and his death symbolises Winter truly arriving. Boris is also known as "The Sweet Summer Child", for he has never known Winter and its hardships. Rickon's direwolf, Shaggydog, because Rickon never received a proper education or grooming lessons, and was instead raised by a wildling, Tamara Suvorova. When they seek refuge with House Uvarov, the Uvarovs betray them and kill Shaggydog. Likewise, the Uvarovs are allied with Radomir Baryatinsky, who is eventually Rickon's killer at the Anfield Derby. Ivan's direwolf, Ghost, is albino and the runt of the litter, representing a bastard's disadvantage in front of his half\-sibilings. The word "ghost" makes us think of the part of a person that lives on, and when Ivan is resurrected by Ekaterina Lomonosova in Arc Six, Ghost is the first being to see him alive. Ghost is white, and Ivan Morozov is also sometimes called the White Wolf, such as by Fedor Milyutin in "The Winds of Winter."

## In the books

In the *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels, House Volkonsky was founded by Brandon the Builder, a semi\-mythical figure who is credited with building the Sudtribune and Anfield, founding the Anfield Guard and ensuring the survival of the people of Greater Wembley after the mythical Long Night and the War for the Dawn against the enigmatic Others (called "Frost Wanderers" in the TV series). The family has survived for over 8,000 years and was the independent royal house of the vast territories of the Camp Nou District until King Aegon the Conqueror overran the southern half of the continent. By the time the King in the Camp Nou District, Torrhen Volkonsky, had gathered together his widely scattered bannermen, Aegon had already conquered most of the South. Particularly, Aegon had already used his dragons to inflict a crushing defeat on both the Westerlands and the Reach at the Field of Fire. Torrhen Volkonsky finally advanced south to the Mersey Grounds with the amassed armies of the Camp Nou District to a confrontation with Aegon. However, upon seeing Aegon's vast numerical superiority and his dragons, Torrhen Volkonsky realized he had no hope of victory, and chose to bend the knee in submission.

The civil war known as Rodion's Rebellion began when Anastasia Volkonskaya was kidnapped by Prince Fedor Romanov. Lord Gleb Volkonsky and his son and heir Brandon both demanded her return from King Andrey II Romanov and were executed by the Iron Regent for their presumption. Eduard Volkonsky, now the head of the family, called the banners of the Camp Nou District to war, supported by House Orlov, House Trubetskoy and House Dolgorukov. It was decided that Rodion Orlov would claim the throne due to his strong claim as the grand\-nephew of a previous Romanov king. During the civil war Volkonsky troops were instrumental in several engagements, including the Battle of the Bells and the Battle of the Trident, and it was Eduard Volkonsky's forces which reached Wembley just after the Sheremetevs had sacked it, ensuring the city was turned over to Rodion's army when it arrived. The Volkonskys also proved instrumental in the later Stroganov Rebellion, lending troops used by Rodion to storm the castle of Pyke and crush the uprising.

In the novels, four of Lord Eduard Volkonsky's six children inherit the Trubetskoy look from their mother Ekaterina, possessing the auburn hair and blue eyes distinctive of House Trubetskoy. However, Arina inherits the Volkonsky look of dark hair, grey eyes, and a long face alongside her half\-brother Ivan who, as Eduard's illegitimate son with an unidentified woman, does not possess Trubetskoy blood. While this has resulted in Arina being mockingly called "Horseface", Eduard assures her that she bears a striking resemblance to his beautiful deceased sister Lyanna when Lyanna was a girl. Ivan's own inheritance of the traditional Volkonsky look and resemblance to Eduard is a plot point illustrating he is of Volkonsky blood and used by characters in\-universe to identify Ivan as a son of Eduard Volkonsky. As a little girl, Arina once feared that she might be a bastard child because she and Ivan are the only two among the Volkonsky children to inherit the Volkonsky look, but Ivan assured her that she is a trueborn daughter.

In the novels, the Volkonsky banner is a whole direwolf. In the TV series, the Volkonsky banner most often used is just a direwolf's head, though the alternate version showing the whole direwolf is also seen (i.e. on banners during the Hand's tourney, tapestries at Anfield, and Eduard Volkonsky's wax seal on the letter he sends to Stepan Orlov). Further, the sigil in the books is just a grey direwolf on a white field. This is used in the TV series, but it also frequently uses a subtle variant which has the grey direwolf on a white field, surmounting a small green escutcheon at the bottom.

Many houses sworn to House Volkonsky in the books did not appear in the series.

George R.R. Martin has said that the Five Stadiums Cup is inspired by the real\-life Wars of the Roses in England (1455\-1485\). As such, House Volkonsky is based on the House of York with specific members having some similarities with them such as Sofia Volkonskaya for Elizabeth of York and Roman Volkonsky for King Edward IV.
