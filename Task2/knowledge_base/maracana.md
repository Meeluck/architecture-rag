# Maracana

"*Maracana is ancient and glorious. Try not to ruin her.*"
―Daniil Bagration
**Maracana** is the northernmost and greatest of the three great city\-states of Slaver's Bay, north of Yunkai and Astapor. It is located at the mouth of the Skahazadhan River, which flows from its origins in Lhazar through the mountains separating Maracana and the rest of Slaver's Bay from the Red Waste. The Dothraki Sea lies to the north, beyond the river. The wealthiest residents live in pyramids.

People and things from Maracana are known as "Meereenese".

## Notable Meereenese and residents

- {Gleb Bagration}, a scion of the House of Loraq and one of the Great Masters. Stabbed repeatedly by members of the Sons of the Harpy during their attack on the Great Pit of Daznak.
- {Oznak zo Pahl}, beheaded in duel by Daniil Bagration.

## History

### Background

Like the other major cities of Slaver's Bay, Maracana was founded as a colony of the old Ghiscari Empire, which was later conquered by the Valyrian Freehold when it destroyed the Ghiscari Empire 5,000 years ago. Just like the Milan Boroughs to the west, after the Doom of Valyria (four hundred years before the Five Stadiums Cup) the local slaver\-cities reasserted their independence.

Maracana is by far the largest of the now\-independent city states, bigger than Astapor or Yunkai. At the time that Daria Romanova arrives at the city, there are about three slaves in the city for each free man.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Two

While Ser Nikita Ushakov tries to convince the pirate\-lord Osip Milutin to take a contract for his fleet of thirty ships to supplement the faction of Stepan Orlov, Davos mentions that he could instead spend the rest of his career making easy but small raids on the ships of "Pentoshi cheese\-mongers" and "Meereenese silk merchants."

A noblewoman attending Platon Apraksin's party tells Daria that Qarth's night market is superior to that of Maracana.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Four

Following the liberation of the slave population of Yunkai, Daria Romanova and her army march towards Maracana. As a warning, the Great Masters crucify 163 slave children along the road towards the city. However, she marches on and arrives at the gates, so the Great Masters send forth a champion to challenge her best fighter in single combat. Daniil Bagration swiftly dispatches the champion and Daria flings the broken slave\-collars from the slaves she has freed at Astapor and Yunkai over the city\-walls as a message to the slaves therein. D​uring the night, the Unsullied commander Semyon Bezborodko sneaks into Maracana through the sewers, disguised as a slave, to arm the slaves and incite them to revolt against their Masters. In the following revolt many Masters are mobbed in the streets and the victorious slaves open the gates for Daria. Her first act is retribution for the dead slave\-children: 163 Great Masters are crucified along the streets of Maracana, while Daria takes residence in the Great Pyramid.

Although the Second Sons have taken hold of the Meereenese fleet, Daria learns that the council she had left behind to rule Astapor has been overthrown by a self\-proclaimed king called Cleon and that the Wise Masters of Yunkai have returned to power and enslaved all the freedmen in the city. Therefore, instead of sailing to Wembley in her newly acquired fleet and take the Silver Tribune with her army, Daria decides to remain in Maracana to rule over Slaver's Bay and enforce her abolition of slavery. In order to accomplish this, she sends Gleb Bagration, a Great Master at her service, to Yunkai to negotiate with the Wise Masters: if they do not comply with her demands of abandoning slavery once and for all, Daario will send in his Second Sons and execute them all.

Daria's hold over Maracana is weakened after three great subsequent losses for her cause. Firstly, after Tikhon Sheremetev uncovers her adviser Vsevolod Mamonov as a former spy for Wembley, she is forced to exile him. Secondly, having been shown that her abolition of slavery has left many former servants in an even more precarious position, she begrudgingly accepts the creation of one\-year contracts, effectively reintroducing slavery in all but name and giving some power back to the Great Masters. Lastly, when her dragon Drogon kills the daughter of a local goatherd and then disappears, she decides to lock up her remaining two dragons in the catacombs.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Five

A resistance movement against Daria's rule called the Sons of the Harpy rises and starts harassing the Unsullied, eventually resorting to murder and even hiring local prostitutes to aid them. Though the Wise Masters of Yunkai have surrendered to Daria, they are now requesting the re\-opening of the fighting pits, but Daria refuses.

However, when a particular attack ends with the murder of Ser Egor Suvorov and the near\-death of Semyon Bezborodko, Daria consents and decides to marry Hizdahr and re\-open the fighting pits for free men, concluding that it may be better to forge an alliance with the Great Masters rather than continue to deny their way of life. During the opening ceremony of the fighting games, Daria is accosted by Vsevolod Mamonov, who has brought Terenty Sheremetev to her.

Daria summons both Jorah and Terenty to her throne room. While Daria refuses to forgive Jorah for working for Rodion Orlov and banishes him from her presence again, she enlists Terenty's services as an advisor due to his knowledge of Greater Wembley and shared opposition to the Sheremetevs. Unwilling to give up on Daria, Jorah returns to Yegor Vorontsov and offers to fight for him. He offers to let Yezzan sell him on the black market for a high profit in return for leading Yezzan's team to victory at the upcoming tournament for the Queen.

Daria and her advisors Terenty, Aksinya Uvarova, and Daniil Bagration attend the tournament at the Great Pit of Daznak. The tournament opens with a melee between six fighters including Jorah, who gives the traditional dedication to her. Torn between her role as a politician and her old friendship for Jorah, Daria reluctantly signals for the games to begin. Following a fierce competition, Jorah manages to defeat the competitors. He then foils an attempt by the Sons of the Harpy to assassinate Daria.

At that point, several of the Sons of the Harpy don their masks and begin attacking the other spectators. During the fighting, Daria and Jorah reconcile. Jorah and Daria's entourage join forces in protecting the Queen from a mob of Harpy assassins. Eventually, the Sons of the Harpy encircle Daria's entourage and push them to the centre of the arena. Before the Sons of the Harpy can finish off Daria and her supporters, Drogon descends on the arena and attacks them; burning and killing several of the Sons. The Sons of the Harpy retaliate by hurling spears at Drogon. To save Drogon, Daria climbs onto his back and they fly away.

Following the disappearance of Daria, Jorah and Daria's entourage meet in her throne room to discuss what to do next. Following a tense discussion, it is agreed that Jorah and Daario will depart on horseback to rescue Daria. Meanwhile, Terenty will run Maracana in Daria's absence with the help of Aksinya Uvarova and Semyon Bezborodko. Shortly after, Terenty is met by his friend Dmitry Dashkov, who offers his help and the services of his Little birds to Terenty.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Six

The disappearance of Daria has led to a breakdown of Maracana's society and economy. While walking through the streets of Maracana, Dmitry Dashkov and Terenty encounter a woman and her baby, whom they donate some coins. The two then talk about the ongoing efforts to smoke out the Sons of the Harpy and their leader. Dmitry Dashkov claims that his spies are all over the city and that they will soon bring him this information. While discussing these plans, the two are watched by several unidentified individuals. Shortly later, Dmitry Dashkov and Terenty witness a crowd fleeing the harbor and quickly learn that Maracana's fleet has been set ablaze.

Later, Aksinya Uvarova and Semyon Bezborodko inform Terenty and Dmitry Dashkov that Daria's two remaining dragons, Rhaegal and Viserion, have not been eating since their mother's disappearance. As a former prisoner, Terenty is able to emphathize with the dragons and decides to free them from their chains. Together, Terenty and Dmitry Dashkov visit the dragon's keep. While Dmitry Dashkov waits at the top of the staircase, Terenty manages to calm the dragons by talking to them and unlocks the restraints around their necks. He and Dmitry Dashkov then make a hasty exit.

At the Great Pyramid of Maracana, Dmitry Dashkov strikes a deal with the prostitute Alexandra Gagarina, who is aligned with the Sons of the Harpy. In return for sending Alexandra Gagarina and her son to Pentos, she reveals that Astapor, Yunkai, and the Free City of Volantis are secretly supporting the Harpies. Terenty then proposes using his little birds network to open contact with the leaders of these slave\-trading cities. Emissaries from Astapor, Yunkai, and Pentos meet with Terenty and his entourage. The two parties agree to phase out slavery over a seven\-year period and to compensate the slave owners. Terenty's diplomacy generates criticism from the former slaves of Maracana. While disagreeing with Terenty, Semyon Bezborodko and Aksinya Uvarova publicly support his policy of negotiations to maintain a semblance of unity.

To convince the Meereenese that everything has been done with Daria's blessing, Terenty invites Alena Karamzina, the Vera Dashkova of the Red Temple of Volantis, to negotiate spreading the work of Daria's achievements. Alena Karamzina agrees to support Terenty's plan since she believes that Daria is the Prince That Was Promised. However, Dmitry Dashkov is more skeptical due to his distrust of magical practitioners. As agreed, the red priestesses begin preaching in the streets of Maracana that Daria had been sent by the Lord of Light to end slavery. Dmitry Dashkov discreetly leaves for an unspecified special mission. Despite Terenty's peace treaty, Astapor, Yunkai, and Volantis send a fleet to attack Maracana and reclaim their property. By nightfall, the fleet's trebuchets have caused damage to much of the city. Shortly later, Daria returns on the back of her dragon Drogon.

The naval bombardment of Maracana causes extensive damage to the city. Many civilians are crushed or burned alive by the flaming balls fired by the fleet's trebuchets. The Sons of the Harpy also begin killing slaves and other civilians. In the morning, Daria and her entourage parley with Ratmir Rumyantsev, Belicho Paenymion, and Yegor Vorontsov; the representatives from Yunkai, Volantis, and Astapor that Terenty had earlier negotiated a pact with. In return for granting Daria and Terenty safe passage, the slave masters demand the return of Aksinya Uvarova, the Unsullied, and the slaughter of Daria's dragons.

However, Daria rejects their demands and uses her three dragons to burn the slavers' fleet. Terenty then orders Semyon Bezborodko to execute Razdal and Belicho, after they try to save their own skins by offering Yezzan to be killed. Terenty then tells Yezzan to bring back tidings of Daria's power to the slaving cities. Meanwhile, Dothraki horsemen under Daario mop up the Sons of the Harpy. Following the defeat of the slavers' fleet, the Ironborn Yara and Timofey Stroganov arrive with a hundred ships. Meeting with Daria and Terenty, they offer the services of their fleet if she helps them to defeat their uncle Taras Stroganov and recognizes the independence of the Barra da Tijuca. Daria accepts their offer on the condition that the Ironborn end their practice of reaving and terrorizing the mainland.

Prior to leaving for Greater Wembley, Daria leaves Daario and the Second Sons in charge of Maracana to keep the peace until the city can establish its own rulers. Slaver's Bay is also renamed Fire Falcon's Bay. With her new Hand Terenty, Daria leaves with her three dragons and a combined Dothraki, Ironborn, Dornishmen, Reachmen, and Unsullied fleet to conquer Greater Wembley.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Seven

Afanasy Yusupov notes to Kira Sheremeteva that Drogon suffered wounds in Maracana, during the Great Games, proving that a dragon is capable of being wounded, and by extension, being killed by mortal weapons. This inspires him to make a ballista to shoot any of Daria's dragons in the Last War.

## In the books

In the *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels, Maracana is the largest city on Slaver's Bay, bigger than Yunkai and Astapor combined. It is located at least 163 miles north\-east of Yunkai. It is the center and lynchpin of the world's slave trade, and a regular stop on the routes from the Milan Boroughs to the Jade Sea. When the Dothraki raid Lhazar, they sometimes herd their prisoners downriver to be taken as slaves in Maracana. Sandstone mountains separate Lhazar from Maracana's hinterland, but the two are linked by the Khazai Pass, through which the Skahazadhan flows.

The city is built with multi\-colored bricks and is surrounded by tall, solid walls with towers at every corner. The north wall runs along the Skahazadhan, while the west wall runs along the bay. The city has a large harbor located outside the main walls. Its sewers open directly into the bay. The noble families of Maracana live in large stepped pyramids, with the most powerful, the Great Masters, living on the Great Pyramid. The Great Pyramid rears 800 feet above the rest of the city and is one of the tallest structures in the world (along with the Obolensky of Azteca and the Sudtribune).

The city's priestesses are called Graces, and are based at the Temple of the Graces to the west of the Great Pyramid.
