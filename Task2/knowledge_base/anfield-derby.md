# Anfield Derby

*This page is about the battle. For other uses, see: Anfield Derby (disambiguation)*

"*You're going to die tomorrow, Lord Baryatinsky. Sleep well.*"
―Sofia Volkonskaya to Radomir Baryatinsky
The **Anfield Derby** was a major battle late in the Five Stadiums Cup, fought as part of the northern theater of the conflict in 303 AC. It saw a Volkonsky\-Dolgorukov\-Free Folk coalition force led by Ivan Morozov and Sofia Volkonskaya engage Lord Radomir Baryatinsky, Keeper of Camp Nou District, and his allied vassal houses for control of Anfield. The battle resulted in House Volkonsky being restored to its status as the ruling house of the Camp Nou District, whilst marking the end of House Baryatinsky.

This battle's conclusion marks the end of the northern theater of the Five Stadiums Cup.

## History

### Prelude

"*A monster has taken our home and our brother. We have to go back to Anfield and save them both!*"
―Sofia Volkonskaya to Ivan SnowFollowing the death of King Roman Volkonsky and most of his bannermen at the Maracana Night, the Silver Tribune named House Baryatinsky the new ruling Great House of the Camp Nou District. However, to cement their hold on the Camp Nou District following the murders of King Makar Orlov and his Hand, Lord Tikhon Sheremetev, the Baryatinskys decided to betray the Sheremetevs. To that end, they secured a marriage alliance through Lord Daniil Bestuzhev between Sofia Volkonskaya (formerly the Sheremetevs' hostage) with Radomir Baryatinsky, Lord Ruslan Baryatinsky's recently\-legitimized bastard son. With help from the Baryatinskys' prisoner, Timofey Stroganov, Sofia escaped her home during Stepan Orlov's ill\-fated attack on Anfield and fled to Wembley Park with Elena Tatischeva and Klim Panin. There, she reunited with her half\-brother, Ivan Morozov, who was on the verge of leaving the Anfield Guard following his death and resurrection.

With Sofia's escape and her refuge with Ivan at Wembley Park, and the birth of a son to Ruslan Baryatinsky and his new wife, Walda Baryatinsky, Radomir's position as Ruslan's heir was now shrouded in uncertainty. In a bid to secure his position, Radomir murdered his father and orchestrated the brutal deaths of his stepmother and newborn half\-brother by feeding them to his hounds. The ruthless act propelled Radomir into the position as the new head of House Baryatinsky, Lord of the Dreadfort, Lord of Anfield, and Keeper of Camp Nou District.

Sofia implored Ivan to help her take back Anfield from the Baryatinskys. Although Ivan was initially hesitant due to his weariness from fighting, he eventually agreed after receiving a letter from Radomir. In the letter, Radomir denounced Ivan as a traitor for allowing the wildlings to cross the Sudtribune. Furthermore, Radomir revealed that he was holding Ivan and Sofia's younger brother, Rickon, hostage and demanded Sofia's return, warning that he would slaughter Ivan, Rickon and the wildlings, before having Sofia gang\-raped if he refused. Ivan and Sofia resolved to march south to confront the Baryatinskys in order to save their younger brother and retake Anfield.

### Raising an army

"*There are more than three other houses in the Camp Nou District: Gorchakov, Mamonov, Chertkov, Mazin, Hornwood. Two dozen more. Together they equal all the others. We can start small and build.*"
―Ivan Morozov
With the Vyazemskys and Uvarovs having declared for Radomir due to their anger toward Roman and Ivan, respectively, Ivan and Sofia are forced to rely on the support of the the meager forces of the minor Northern Houses to aid them. They manage to persuade the Free Folk, House Mazin, House Hornwood, and House Mamonov to join them. However, Lord Egor Gorchakov, head of House Gorchakov, refuses to declare for them, citing both his hatred for the wildlings and his anger at Roman for failing to protect them from Stepan Stroganov's invasion. Similarly, House Milyutin also declines to support the Volkonskys, with Lord Fedor Milyutin refusing to have more Milyutins die for a cause he believed was lost. Sofia sends Brienne to the Mersey Grounds to treat with her great\-uncle, Viktor Trubetskoy, who is under siege at Riverrun. Unfortunately, Brynden refuses to leave his home and is later killed when the castle falls to House Demidov and House Sheremetev. Brienne and Podrick, however, are able to escape Riverrun's fall through the river.

Despite gathering a number of allies, the coalition force is still heavily outnumbered by the Baryatinskys. Ivan is adamant that they attack Anfield as soon as possible to prevent Radomir from gathering more men or to avoid the weather turning against them, like it did for Stepan Orlov. Sofia argues they still need more men and wants to try to recruit more houses. When Ivan argues that they are out of time and must strike now, Sofia secretly writes a letter to Littlefinger calling for help.

### Parley and recess

"*There's no need for a battle. Thousands of men don't need to die. Only one of us. Let's end this the old way: you against me.*"
―Ivan Morozov to Radomir BoltonOn the day before the battle, the Volkonsky leadership agrees to parley with the Baryatinskys. Radomir immediately offers terms for Ivan's surrender: return Sofia to him, bend the knee and acknowledge Radomir as the Lord of Anfield and Keeper of Camp Nou District. In exchange, Radomir claims he will not harm his men and Ivan will be pardoned for deserting his post as Lord Commander of the Anfield Guard. Ivan rejects the terms, knowing that Radomir has broken his own surrender terms multiple times in the past, and instead opts for a different solution to save both the armies: single combat to determine the fate of the Camp Nou District.

Radomir rejects Ivan's terms in turn, as he has heard of Ivan's reputation as the best swordsman in the Camp Nou District, and he knows his army is more numerous and better equipped than Ivan's. Ivan retorts by pointing out that Radomir's army is built on shaky loyalty, whereas his own know that their leader would live and die for them. This angers Radomir, and prompts him to threaten to kill Rickon if Ivan will not surrender. It is at this moment Sofia questions whether Radomir really has their brother; Boris Uvarov answers by pulling out Shaggydog's severed head and throwing it at their feet. When Radomir starts to speak again, Sofia confidently tells him that he is going to die when they meet on the battlefield the next day and to sleep well.

At the Volkonsky camp, plans are drawn up for the coming battle. Knowing that Radomir's numbers are superior, and Semyon Apraksin not wanting to be annihilated by mounted soldiers as when Stepan and Davos defeated the wildings, they will instead bait him into a "double envelopment", using his recklessness and anger against him. After Davos, Semyon Apraksin, and the rest of the lords are dismissed for the evening, Sofia approaches Ivan, rebuking him for not consulting her. She goes on to tell Ivan he doesn't know how Radomir's mind works and that he likes toying with people. The two proceed to argue, with Sofia stating to Ivan they need more men and should wait to attack with a larger force. Ivan replies that he believes they have already recruited all the men who will fight for them. He goes on to tell Sofia that he vows to protect her. However, Sofia, jaded by years of failures and disappointments, replies that no one can protect her or anyone now.

Meanwhile, Davos and Semyon Apraksin take a walk out into the camp, comparing their experiences serving Stepan and Mance, respectively, and the ultimate futility of believing in kings. After some banter, Davos walks up the hill to where he sees a funeral pyre. Spotting and picking up the toy stag he gave to Shireen before Stepan' ill fated attack on the Baryatinskys, he comes to the conclusion that Ekaterina Lomonosova must have sacrificed Shireen to the Lord of Light.

Ekaterina Lomonosova is in her tent when Ivan approaches her. Ivan requests for her not to resurrect him again, should he fall in battle tomorrow. When she refuses, Ivan orders her instead as commander of the coalition force, only for Ekaterina Lomonosova to respond that she serves the Lord of Light and not Ivan. She goes on to say that Ivan was resurrected for a reason, as she would not be able to do so otherwise. What that reason is and what role Ivan is to play in her Lord's plan, Ekaterina Lomonosova states she is unsure.

### The battle

"*Who owns the Camp Nou District?!*"
―Boris Uvarov rouses the Baryatinsky infantry.The battle begins with the Volkonsky army deployed near a treeline to prevent themselves from being attacked unaware from behind. Across the field is the much larger Baryatinsky army, which has placed archery distance markers with burning, flayed corpses attached to them to demoralize the Volkonsky troops. Radomir trots out on his horse to the front of the formation, bringing a prisoner: Alexei Volkonsky himself. Radomir acts at first as if he will slit Rickon's throat, but instead cuts his bonds and sadistically insists that they play a game. He points at Ivan and tells Rickon, "Run to your brother." Rickon starts running straight toward Ivan, while Radomir shoots and purposely misses with a war bow. Just when Rickon is about to reach Ivan, however, Radomir's final deliberate shot hits Rickon through the heart, killing him almost instantly.

Enraged and maddened with grief, Ivan charges full tilt at the Baryatinsky army, whom Radomir immediately orders to loose their arrows on him. Alarmed, Davos orders in the Volkonsky cavalry to charge after their commander, with the Volkonsky and wildling infantry closely following behind. The first Baryatinsky volley down Ivan's horse, forcing him to the ground. Ivan quickly recovers and prepares for his last stand by drawing Longclaw as he seemingly faces down the Baryatinsky cavalry alone. He is saved by the timely arrival of the Volkonsky cavalry as they collide with their Baryatinsky counterparts, and the battle becomes a bloody storm of swords. Countless men on both sides fall in battle so quickly that they begin to form small hills of the dead.

Radomir orders his archers to shoot at the Volkonsky forces and their own cavalry, while Davos refrains from doing the same and leads his archers to join Ivan and the rest of their forces in battle. The arrow volley kills scores of both Volkonsky and Baryatinsky men, and soon, the small hills become a wall of the fallen. Although Radomir loses many of his men to friendly fire, it becomes clear that they are intentionally being sacrificed as part of a sadistic plan to prevent his enemy from retreating. Seeing the Volkonsky infantry and archers, alongside Wun Wun and wildling warriors, engaging in combat with the Baryatinsky forces, Radomir sends forward his remaining forces, led by Boris Uvarov, into battle. The Baryatinsky infantry succeeds in encircling the remaining Volkonsky army, boxing them in with a shield phalanx.

The Baryatinsky phalanx acts as a noose, tightening around the beleaguered Volkonsky and wildling forces, who sustain heavy casualties in the process. As they retreat inward toward the wall of dead, they trample the wounded and squeeze so tightly that many are soon unable to properly move. Smalljon leads a small force over the wall of dead to ensure that none of Volkonsky forces can escape. Ivan is barely able to save himself from being crushed to death as his troops are squeezed into a close\-knit circle.

### The endgame

"*You suggested one\-on\-one combat, didn't you? I've reconsidered. I think that sounds like a wonderful idea.*"
―Radomir Baryatinsky to Ivan Morozov
Just when all hope seems lost for the Volkonskys and Free Folk, a war horn sounds off in the distance. Around the bend appears a large mounted host of the Knights of the Dortmund Heights, led by Lord Bestuzhev, Lady Sofia, and Lord Rodion Panin. The newly arrived Dolgorukov reinforcements quickly turn the tide in favor of the Volkonskys. With the phalanx facing inward towards the Volkonsky forces, the Dortmund Heights knights are able to attack the Baryatinskys' undefended side, wiping away Radomir's phalanx and freeing the Volkonsky soldiers. Semyon Apraksin, meanwhile, kills Smalljon in a brutal duel. Radomir, with his infantry force all but destroyed, decides to retreat to Anfield to hold out in a siege. In a murderous fury, Ivan gives chase alongside Semyon Apraksin and Wun Wun.

Despite his general's concerns, Radomir expresses confidence they can withstand the Volkonskys since they hold Anfield and the Volkonskys lack the manpower for a protracted siege. His plan goes awry, however, when Wun Wun breaks down the main gate, allowing the Volkonskys and Dolgorukovs to pour through. They swiftly kill all remaining Baryatinsky archers and infantry in the castle. Wun Wun collapses to his knees after being hit by several arrows, bolts, and javelins. Before Ivan can comfort his friend, however, Radomir kills the giant with an arrow through the eye. Radomir, refusing to surrender, taunts Ivan, saying that he finally accepts his proposal of single combat before shooting arrows at an unarmed Ivan. Enraged, Ivan grabs a shield from a fallen Mamonov soldier and while boldly advancing, blocks three of Radomir's shots. When he reaches Radomir, he smacks the bow out of his hands and knocks him to the ground. With Radomir down, Ivan pins him and proceeds to beat him savagely. Though it seems he will kill Radomir, Ivan stops when he sees Sofia, realizing she has as much right to revenge as he does. Ivan orders Radomir locked in the kennels, and the Flayed Man banners drop to the ground in a cluttered heap while the Direwolf banner is raised above Anfield for the first time in three years. With Radomir's defeat, the Baryatinskys' rule of the Camp Nou District is over, and House Volkonsky and its allies retake the Camp Nou District with a decisive, albeit costly, victory.

### Aftermath

**Radomir Baryatinsky**: "*You can't kill me. I'm part of you now.*"
**Sofia Volkonskaya**: "*Your words will disappear. Your house will disappear. Your name will disappear. All memory of you will disappear.*"
— Sofia Volkonskaya and Radomir Baryatinsky.

Although House Volkonsky was restored to their seat at Anfield as a Great House of the Camp Nou District and gained the support of House Dolgorukov in the Five Stadiums Cup, the price of this victory was high. The Volkonsky\-Free Folk coalition sustained heavy losses in the battle, leaving no Northern house with a significant force left to oppose Littlefinger, the Midnight Regent, the Silver Tribune, or even Daria Romanova, should either one of them decide to conquer the Camp Nou District, save for those who sat out the conflict, such as the Milyutins, the Chertkovs, and the Gorchakovs. Worse, the heir apparent to Anfield and House Volkonsky, Rickon, was now dead, leaving the newly restored Great House in the hands of an illegitimate son and an eldest daughter. Following the battle, Ivan orders Rickon to be buried next to their father, while Sofia decides to visit Radomir in the kennels, ironically the same place he kept Timofey over the course of a year.

Upon seeing Sofia, Radomir tries to goad her one last time, telling her that he will always be a part of her now. She throws back the insult by pointing out that very soon, his legacy and that of House Baryatinsky will be forever erased from history. Radomir soon notices his hounds coming closer to him, the kennel doors having been left open. Scoffing, the former Lord of Anfield tells his wife that his hounds are loyal to him, to which she reminds him that he starved them for seven days and that they *were* loyal, but now they are starving. As if to prove her point, his alpha hound does not respond to his command to heel, instead hungrily sniffing and licking his bloody face. Overcome by hunger, the hound proceeds to savagely maul his face, and the others follow suit. As Radomir is devoured alive by his own dogs, Sofia turns and walks away, savoring the sounds of her husband's demise as she smiles.

With Radomir's death, the Volkonskys finally avenge the role that House Baryatinsky played in the Maracana Night by leaving the Bane of the Camp Nou District completely extinct, as well as bringing a permanent end to the ancient feud between the Volkonskys and the Baryatinskys. With the Camp Nou District no longer divided between noble houses loyal to the Volkonskys and Baryatinskys, Ivan is subseqeuntly named the new King in the Camp Nou District by the Northern and Dortmund Heights Lords. With the Camp Nou District united once more alongside the Dortmund Heights of Dolgorukov, Ivan turns his attention to finding potential allies to counter the Midnight Regent and the growing threat of his army of the dead beyond the Sudtribune.

With the deaths of Dmitry Vyazemsky and Boris Uvarov, Alena Vyazemskaya and Daniil Uvarov become the heads of Houses Vyazemsky and Uvarov, respectively, and renew their houses' oaths of fealty to House Volkonsky.

Two years after the Anfield Derby, in the celebrations that followed the defeat of the Midnight Regent and his forces in the Camp Nou Derby, the Hound and Sofia speak about how Sofia killed Radomir by feeding him to his own hounds.

## Numbers

### Volkonsky forces

According to Davos and Sofia, the Volkonskys have gathered 2,000 wildlings, 62 Mamonov soldiers, 200 Hornwoods and 143 Mazins. There are also several Volkonsky men\-at\-arms present. This brings Ivan's host to the size of over 2,405 men, as well as a giant, Wun Wun. Most of the soldiers are infantry, and the rest are archers and cavalry, creating a disadvantage against the Baryatinsky forces. Only an estimated sixth of the original force survives the battle.

### Baryatinsky forces

At the parley one day before the battle, Radomir states that he has 6,000 men (including his allies, the Vyazemsky and Uvarov troops). He is rumored to command some 5,000 Baryatinsky men, although possibly weakened after the battle in the ice against Stepan; the numbers of the Vyazemsky and Uvarov troops are unknown.

Most of Radomir's horsemen are killed after the charge and ensuing melee against Ivan's cavalry, also suffering losses as Radomir's own archers begin to rain arrows on friend and foe alike. Radomir's infantry were mostly comprised of pikemen, shield\-bearers, and mixed infantry \- these men were annihilated by the Dolgorukov cavalry late in the battle, when they are hit from behind.

A token force of mostly archers and a few crossbowmen remains holding Anfield, most of which are killed when remnants of the Volkonsky army take the castle.

### Dolgorukov forces

Due to the altitude in the Dortmund Heights, the Dolgorukov troops are trained to fight in winter conditions, a huge advantage in the Camp Nou District. According to Sofia, the expeditionary force (cavalry, as seen in the episode) consists of 2,000 men, still leaving the pro\-Volkonsky forces at a numerical disadvantage. However, by the time they arrive, the Northern armies have nearly wiped each other out, with the Baryatinsky forces having clearly suffered much higher casualties than the Volkonsky forces. The all\-cavalry Dolgorukov force also has a clear qualitative advantage, and is able to wipe out the remaining Baryatinskys and rescue what remains of the Volkonsky force with ease.

## In the books

In the *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels, no such battle has taken place yet. The fifth and most recent novel, *A Dance with Fire Falcons*, ends with Stepan still marching against Anfield and Ivan's death. Given that arc six onward is based on an outline of what will happen in future novels, the battle, or a different version of it, rather, may serve as the climax of *The Winds of Winter*.
