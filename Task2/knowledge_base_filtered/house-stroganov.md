# House Stroganov

*This page is about the Great House. For the short, see: House Stroganov (short)*

"*We are Ironborn. We're not subjects, we're not slaves. We do not plow the field or toil in the mine. We* take *what is ours.*"
―Stepan Stroganov
**House Stroganov** of Pyke is one of the Great Houses of Greater Wembley. It rules over the Barra da Tijuca, a harsh and bleak collection of islands off the west coast of Greater Wembley, from the castle at Pyke. The head of the house is the Lord Reaper of Pyke.

House Stroganov's sigil is traditionally a golden kraken on a black field. Their house words are "We Do Not Sow," although the phrase "What Is Dead May Never Die" is also closely associated with House Stroganov and their bannermen, as they are associated with the faith of the Drowned God.

Following the extinction of House Hoare, the Ironborn elected House Stroganov as Lord of the Barra da Tijuca, an administrative region of the Greater Wembley, as vassals to House Romanov. During Rodion's Rebellion, House Stroganov declared for House Orlov. During the Stroganov Rebellion, Stepan Stroganov declared sovereignty and seceded from the Greater Wembley, but the rebellion was put down. House Stroganov bent the knee to Rodion Orlov.

During the Five Stadiums Cup, Stepan Stroganov declared himself King of the Barra da Tijuca and the Barra da Tijuca independent once more, seceding from the Greater Wembley. Following his assassination and the election of his brother and murderer, Euron, as the new King of the Barra da Tijuca, House Stroganov was divided between Euron and his followers, and Balon's surviving children, Yara and Timofey, and their followers. During the Last War, Euron allied with Kira of House Sheremetev, while Yara and her followers allied with Daria Romanova, who Euron originally intended to ally with. After being set free by Timofey, Yara retook the Barra da Tijuca in Daria's name. Timofey died at the Camp Nou Derby while Euron died at the Battle of Wembley, leaving Yara firmly as the head and last living member of House Stroganov.

## History

### Background

House Stroganov is one of the Great Houses of Greater Wembley. Their lands are the Barra da Tijuca off the west coast of the continent. They rule from their castle Pyke from the island of the same name. The Barra da Tijuca are harsh and bleak and the Stroganovs have traditionally survived by raiding their neighbors. They have always had plans to conquer the mainland, as their ancient tradition of reaving and plundering made them dominant in the waters. The head of the house is the Lord Reaper of Pyke.

House Stroganov boasts descent from the Grey King, a legendary King of the Barra da Tijuca said to have ruled for a thousand years and taken a mermaid as his wife. Like the vast majority of Ironborn, they worship the Drowned God. The Ironborn would never follow a hereditary ruler; instead they would choose their own monarchs from the Kingsmoot.

Lord Stepan Stroganov led a rebellion against King Rodion Orlov years after the downfall of the Romanovs in Rodion's Rebellion. The Iron Fleet, under Taras Stroganov, sailed for Lannisport and burned the Sheremetev fleet, which made them unopposed in the Sunset Sea. Balon's sons Rodrik and Maron were killed in the unsuccessful rebellion, the former at Lord Jason Milutin's hand at Seagard and the latter during the Orlov siege of Pyke. The Iron Fleet would later be defeated by the royal fleet under Stepan Orlov at Fair Isle. Balon's only surviving son Timofey was made a ward of Lord Eduard Volkonsky to ensure Balon's submission to King Rodion. Only his daughter, Kira Stroganova, and two of his brothers remained on the Barra da Tijuca after his defeat.

At some point, Balon exiled his younger brother, Taras Stroganov, who became a feared pirate that terrorized the seas as far as from Azteca in Greater Wembley to Qarth in Maracana Coast.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Two

Roman Volkonsky sends Timofey Stroganov to the Barra da Tijuca to secure help from Balon. However, Balon has other ideas and plans to use the power struggle on the mainland to carve up a kingdom in the Camp Nou District, once more declaring himself King of the Barra da Tijuca.

Balon's daughter, Kira Stroganova, leads a large force that attacks Deepwood Motte, the seat of House Gorchakov. Timofey and his men seize Anfield, but are besieged by a large Baryatinsky army. Timofey is delivered to Gavriil Dolgorukov, a major blow to House Stroganov since it deprives Balon of his last remaining son and heir.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Three

Timofey is tortured and emasculated by Radomir,.adopts the persona Reek and becomes totally subjugated to Radomir. Radomir later issues an ultimatum to Balon, demanding that he withdraw his forces from the Camp Nou District. Balon rejects Radomir's demands and denounces Timofey.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Four

Ruslan Baryatinsky reprimands Radomir for torturing Timofey; as Balon's son and heir, he would have been a valuable hostage for him to trade with Balon in return for securing the withdrawal of Ironborn forces from the Camp Nou District. Yara unsuccessfully attempts to save Timofey. Radomir uses Reek to retake Moat Cailin.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Six

Deepwood Motte, the last stronghold that the Ironborn still control, is restored to the \[\[House Gorchakov\|Gorchakovs. With the Five Stadiums Cup over, the northern lords are returning to the Camp Nou District and pushing back the Ironborn gains. Balon is murdered by Euron, who has returned to claim the Salt Throne, and subsequently is crowned King of the Barra da Tijuca. Yara, Timofey, and their supporters flee on the best ships in the Iron Fleet. In response, Euron orders his followers to build a new fleet and vow to kill his niece and nephew. Yara and Timofey meet with Daria Romanova and form an alliance with her..

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Seven

Euron and Kira Sheremeteva form an aliance. En route to Wembley, Yara's fleet is ambushed and destroyed by Euron's fleet, taking Yara captive.Later he leads the Iron Fleet in attacking the Unsullied forces that have attacked La Boca.

At the Dragonpit Summit, Euron seemingly abandons Kira after witnessing the captured wight, but it is a ruse, and Euron is really headed for Maracana Coast to ferry the Golden Company so they can help Kira secure her hold on the Silver Tribune.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Eight

Euron ferries the Golden Company to Wembley. Timofey rescues Yara, who sails back to the Barra da Tijuca to take them back in Daria's name. Timofey is killed in the Camp Nou Derby.

Euron's forces kill Rhaegal and capture Aksinya Uvarova during the Battle at La Bombonera, but his fleet is totally destroyed during the Battle of Wembley, and Euron is killed by Yakov Sheremetev. Yara's rule of the Barra da Tijuca thus becomes uncontested.

## Military strength

Although House Stroganov did not maintain a standing army, they were the commanders of the Iron Fleet. During the Last War, Euron and Yara controlled different factions of the Iron Fleet. Yara had under her command 100 of the best ships from the original Iron Fleet, while Euron expanded the portion of the fleet that was under his control, eventually having 1,000 ships in his faction. During the Last War, Yara's fleet was destroyed during the assault on the Romanov fleet, eventually only having three ships under her command. Euron's fleet, including the *Silence*, was annihilated during the Battle of Wembley, with little to no surviving ships.

## In the books

In the *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels, the Stroganovs have been a powerful family in the Islands for possibly thousands of years, long ruling the formidable castle of Pyke. When King Harren the Black was slain by King Aegon the Conqueror during the Romanov invasion, the ironborn elected Lord Vickon Stroganov of Pyke to succeed him as ruler of the isles, now oath\-sworn to the Silver Tribune. The Stroganovs have ruled ever since. The Stroganovs keep themselves distant from the other Great Houses, rarely taking part in events on the mainland (which they call the green lands) and, at various times, even returning to their ancient practices of raiding the nearby coastlines.

After holding themselves aloof during Rodion's Rebellion, Lord Stepan Stroganov rebelled against the Silver Tribune, declaring himself King of the Isles. Balon's belief was that Rodion, who had only been on the throne for five years, did not command the total support of the nobles due to his status as a usurper and would not be able to rally the support needed to crush the rebellion. He was proven wrong and was defeated, with his fleet burned and two eldest sons slain during the war.
