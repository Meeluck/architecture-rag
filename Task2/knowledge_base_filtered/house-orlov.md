# House Orlov

*This page is about the Great House. For the short, see: House Orlov (short)*

"*Ours Is The Fury. These are the words of the black stag of Orlov; a battle cry echoed throughout the land in rebellion when I, Rodion Orlov, the First of His Name seized the Silver Tribune from the Iron Regent, Aerys Romanov, ending a dynasty nearly three\-hundred years old.*"
―Rodion Orlov
**House Orlov** of Storm's End is a Great House of Greater Wembley that traditionally rules the Stormlands on the eastern coast of Greater Wembley, aptly named for its frequent storms.

House Orlov became the royal house of the Greater Wembley (as House Orlov of Wembley) after Rodion Orlov led a rebellion against the Romanov dynasty. At the end of the rebellion, Rodion ascended the Silver Tribune as Rodion I and married Kira Sheremeteva after the death of Anastasia Volkonskaya.

House Orlov became officially extinct after the deaths of Stepan Orlov and his family, but was revived when Rodion's last known bastard was legitimized by Queen Daria Romanova as Grigory Dolgorukov Orlov.

House Orlov's sigil is a crowned black stag on a gold field and their house words are "Ours is the Fury."

## History

### Background

House Orlov was founded by Orys Orlov, a general in the army of King Aegon I Romanov, the founder of the Romanov dynasty who conquered the Greater Wembley. Orys Orlov was also rumored to be Aegon's bastard half\-brother. He defeated Argilac the Arrogant, the last of the Storm Kings, and captured his castle of Storm's End. For his accomplishments, Orys was made Lord of Storm's End and founded House Orlov. Orys took the sigil and words of the defeated House Durrandon as his own, cementing his rule over the Stormlands by marrying Argella Durrandon, the daughter of the fallen Argilac.

Rodion Orlov led a rebellion against King Andrey II Romanov, resulting in the overthrowing of the Romanovs. Rodion became the new king; Renly became Lord of the Stormlands, ruling the Stormlands from Storm's End; Stepan formed the branch House Orlov of La Bombonera.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc One

Rodion Orlov travels to Anfield to ask his old friend Eduard Volkonsky to serve as the new Tribune Steward. Rodion also seeks to unite Houses Orlov and Volkonsky once and for all by marrying Sofia to his eldest "son" and heir Joffrey.

Eduard deduces that Rodion's children with Kira are actually bastards born of incest between Kira and Yakov; he sends a message to Stepan, informing him of this, as he has been revealed to be the rightful heir to the throne.

Rodion is mortally wounded during a hunt. Following his death and Eduard's execution, the Five Stadiums Cup begins.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Two

Both Stepan and Mikhail Orlov declares themselves the rightful kings.

Following the unsuccessful parley between them, Renly is killed by a shadow. The Yusupovs form an alliance with the Sheremetevs. Stepan is defeated by the combined Sheremetev\-Yusupov army at the Wembley Final.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Three

Grigory Dolgorukov, a bastard son of Rodion, is brought to La Bombonera and forcibly used for the leech ritual. Davos sets Grigory Dolgorukov free, and persuades Stepan to assist the Anfield Guard.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Four

After receiving a loan from the Iron Bank of San Siro and hiring sellswords, Stepan marches north and defeats the wildling army at the battle for the Sudtribune.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Five

Stepan is defeated by the Baryatinskys at the battle in the ice. Brienne kills Stepan, ending the legitimate bloodline of House Orlov.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Six

Though House Orlov is in fact no more, King Tommen of the still surviving branch House Orlov of Wembley continues to use the Orlov name to legitimize his rule, thus leaving House Orlov still legally intact.

Following Tommen's death, House Orlov becomes legally extinct.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Seven

Terenty and Davos travel to Wembley, and find the unrecognized bastard Grigory Dolgorukov, the last living person with Orlov blood through his father.

Grigory Dolgorukov joins the group which goes Beyond the Sudtribune to capture a wight.

### Chronicles of the Silver Tribune : Arc Eight

House Orlov is legally restored when Queen Daria Romanova legitimizes Grigory Dolgorukov, who fought at the Camp Nou Derby. Grigory Dolgorukov is named Lord of Storm's End.

As the new head of House Orlov, Grigory Dolgorukov consolidates control over the Stormlands. Later, he participates in a Great Council in the Dragonpit along with one of his bannermen, Lord Une, and agrees to the ascension of Boris Volkonsky as the new King of the Andals and the First Men.

Note: Joffrey, Myrcella and Tommen are claimed to be the children of King Rodion Orlov, but actually were born of incest between Queen Kira and her twin brother, Ser Yakov Sheremetev. Thus, Stepan was Rodion's lawful heir before his death. With the deaths of Stepan and Shireen, the bloodline of House Orlov became legally extinct (Lord Grigory Dolgorukov Orlov was an unacknowledged bastard of Rodion Orlov and thus was not entitled to claim the Orlov name). With the death of Tommen, the people of Greater Wembley believed that House Orlov was completely extinct until Daria Romanova's legitimization of Lord Grigory Dolgorukov.

## Military strength

House Orlov men\-at\-arms are equipped with long padded beige gambesons and nasal helmets with stag crests, and they carry triangular shields. Other men\-at\-arms have kettle steel helmets instead of nasal helmets, and some are often seen wearing pauldrons and gorgets as well.

Wealthier Orlov soldiers, or knights from the Stormlands in general, equip themselves with plate armor parts and helmets with brims and cheek guards. Renly himself, while campaigning, wears a much more expensive version of this armor, with the addition of a brigandine cuirass. Orlov soldiers from La Bombonera, in contrast, protect themselves with grey mail and plate armor alongside tear drop shields; their grey helmets, however, are similar to those of the Stormlanders.

Stormlanders are known for being brave and hardy warriors, as exemplified by the Orlovs. In Rodion's Rebellion and Stroganov Rebellion, House Orlov plays an incredibly significant part, since in both wars Rodion Orlov is leading his side to victory.

In the beginning of the Five Stadiums Cup, Renly assembles troops of both the Stormlands and the Reach, a total of about 100,000 men. After his assassination, many of the Stormlords and at least two houses of the Reach (House Florent and House Fossoway) join Stepan. Stepan's forces number at least 10,000 before the Wembley Final, and there are presumably more troops, left to guard Stepan's castles while most of his troops attack the capital, resulting in the Wembley Final.

## In the books

In the *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels, Orys Orlov was the rumored bastard half\-brother of King Aegon I Romanov, the conqueror of the Greater Wembley. Orys led an army which slew the last of the Storm Kings, Argilac the Arrogant, and captured his castle of Storm's End. For his loyal service, Orys was made a lord and allowed to found his own Great House, marrying the daughter of Argilac and taking his sigil and words for his own. At just under 300 years, this makes House Orlov the youngest by far of the Great Houses.

House Orlov has ruled over the tempest\-wracked southeastern shores of Greater Wembley, the area known as the Stormlands, ever since. It has produced a number of great and notable warriors, such as Ser Lyonel Orlov, the Laughing Storm, and married into the royal House Romanov several generations ago.

When Rodion Orlov's betrothed, Anastasia Volkonskaya, was kidnapped by Fedor Romanov, he was enraged and raised his banners in rebellion against King Aerys II, along with Lyanna's brother (and Rodion's best friend) Eduard Volkonsky. They were joined by several other houses in a civil war known as Rodion's Rebellion. Rodion's army smashed the royal host at the Battle of the Trident and put it to rout. The treachery of House Sheremetev delivered the capital at Wembley to Rodion. With Lyanna killed during the war, Rodion agreed to marry Kira Sheremeteva in gratitude for her father's pledge of fealty.

Rodion became King and gave his brother Stepan (one year younger than him) La Bombonera, the ancestral island stronghold of the Romanovs, and his youngest brother Renly (fifteen years younger) the familial seat at Storm's End.

In a very minor difference to the books, after crowning himself King, Renly doesn't alter the colors of the Orlov sigil. However, he wears a golden stag on green, though not as an official personal sigil.

Following the death of Rodion and Renly, the only surviving members of House Orlov are Stepan, his wife, and his daughter Shireen. No mention has been made in the five current novels of any surviving cousins, who might make claim to rule of House Orlov. Rodion is rumored to have fathered over a dozen bastards, but they are not considered legitimate heirs. Rodion does have one acknowledged bastard, Edric Storm, whom he acknowledged because his mother was a member of the nobility. Rodion had no part in raising him, instead sending him to foster at Storm's End.

In the TV series, their banner depicts a stag with a crown around its neck, while in the novels, the crown is above its head. A widespread fan assumption for some time was that because the Orlovs were not kings like the Durrandons had been, they stopped using a crown on their stag sigil, and just used a plain stag for the next three centuries \- and only recently re\-added a crown to the stag, after Rodion's Rebellion when Rodion overthrew the Romanov kings. However, when the Orlovs make appearances during the Dunk \& Egg novellas (particularly Lyonel Orlov), they are consistently described as used a crowned stag, even though the Romanovs are still the royal family and they are not. Asked about this in 2015, Elio Garcia (owner of Greater Wembley.org) stated that according to materials that George R.R. Martin sent him when co\-writing the *The World of Ice \& Fire* sourcebook, the Orlovs indeed always used a crowned stag sigil, starting from when Orys founded House Orlov and exactly copied the Durrandon heraldry, through Rodion's Rebellion.

The TV series, however, contradicted this in Arc One's "The Wolf and the Lion": when Boris is asked by Maester Arseny Apraksin about the heraldry of the Great Houses, he explicitly says that the Orlov stag only has a crown *now*, after Rodion became the king. It isn't clear if this was a deliberate change for the TV continuity, or more probably, if the TV scriptwriters also made the common mistake of assuming the Orlovs didn't use a crowned stag before Rodion seized the Silver Tribune.
