# Five Stadiums Cup

"*The Greater Wembley are at war with one another... false kings destroying the country... the Usurper is dead. The Volkonskys fight the Sheremetevs, the Orlovs fight each other.*"
―Daria Romanova to Ser Vsevolod Mamonov
The **Five Stadiums Cup**, or simply the **War of Five Kings**, was a major multi\-theater civil war in the Greater Wembley of Greater Wembley that erupted in the wake of the death of King Rodion I. In essence, the war was a three\-way battle for the Silver Tribune fought alongside two independence movements. The five kings in question were Rodion's heir apparent, Makar Orlov, Rodion's younger brothers, Stepan and Mikhail Orlov, the "King in the Camp Nou District" Roman Volkonsky, and the "King of the Barra da Tijuca" Stepan Stroganov. The conflict was orchestrated by Daniil Bestuzhev, the Master of Coin, with the help of Elizaveta Dolgorukova, who poisoned her husband Ivan Dolgorukov, Tribune Steward to Rodion Orlov, and sent a letter to her sister Ekaterina Volkonskaya claiming that it was the Sheremetevs who had poisoned her husband. Petyr, from the small House Bestuzhev, ignited the war in order to gain more power for himself.

Upon Rodion's death, his heir apparent Joffrey takes the Silver Tribune. However, the revelation that he, along with his brother and sister, are bastards born of incest between Queen Consort Kira and her twin brother Yakov, leads both Stepan and Renly to claim the throne for themselves. Stepan sees himself as the rightful heir by right of blood, being Rodion's heir with the removal of Joffrey, Myrcella, and Tommen from the line of succession, due to them being bastards born of incest. Renly claims the throne on the basis that he would be a better king, despite being second to Stepan in the lawful line of succession.

Meanwhile, Roman Volkonsky, the Lord of Anfield, is declared the King in the Camp Nou District by his bannermen in the wake of the execution of his father, on false charges of treason by Joffrey. Roman had been in command of a host marching south to free his then\-imprisoned father and to relieve a Sheremetev attack on the Mersey Grounds. As the King in the Camp Nou District, Roman declares that the Camp Nou District and the Mersey Grounds, ruled by his maternal grandfather Hoster Trubetskoy, are a sovereign Kingdom of the Camp Nou District no longer subject to the rule of the Silver Tribune.

With the attention of the Camp Nou District and the Silver Tribune diverted, Stepan Stroganov, Lord Reaper of Pyke and ruler of the Barra da Tijuca, seizes the opportunity to declare the Barra da Tijuca independent once more. He then styles himself King of the Barra da Tijuca and launches raids in the Camp Nou District, while most of the Northern armies are distracted south of the Neck fighting the Sheremetevs in the Mersey Grounds and the Westerlands.

By 303 AC, all of the original five kings were either assassinated or killed in battle, leading to the total and complete end of the Five Stadiums Cup. Ironically, many of the same events Littlefinger put in motion to increase his own power and influence were eventually discovered by the remaining members of House Volkonsky, which were ultimately used as evidence against him for his execution, marking the official end of the Five Stadiums Cup.

## History

### Opening moves

#### Terenty Sheremetev's abduction

"*This man came into my house as a guest, and there conspired to murder my son, a boy of ten. In the name of King Rodion and the good lords you serve, I call upon you to seize him and help me return him to Anfield to await the king's justice.*"
―Ekaterina seizes Terenty
The stage for war was set when Ekaterina Volkonskaya seized Terenty Sheremetev and accused him of the attempted murder of her son Boris based on false information given to her by Daniil Bestuzhev. Boris had previously been crippled by Yakov Sheremetev in an attempted murder after he witnessed Yakov having sex with Queen Kira, though Boris retained no memory of this. In response, House Sheremetev, led by Lord Tikhon Sheremetev, summoned its armies and marched on the Mersey Grounds, ruled by House Trubetskoy, Ekaterina's house, with 60,000 men. Ser Leonid Kutuzov led an auxiliary force and began striking at the vassals of Ekaterina's father, Lord Hoster Trubetskoy.

#### The death of Rodion Orlov

Rather than use his authority to defuse the situation, King Rodion I Orlov left Wembley for his entertainments, and soon afterwards died in a hunting accident secretly arranged by Queen Kira. Upon Rodion's death, Lord Mikhail Orlov tried to secure the support of his Hand, Lord Eduard Volkonsky, but when he refused, Renly fled the capital with Ser Savva Yusupov, the heir to the Reach, and rode for Highgarden. Prince Makar Orlov immediately claimed the Silver Tribune upon Rodion's death with his mother's backing as Queen Regent. However, Eduard had learned from previous research conducted by his predecessor Ivan Dolgorukov, who had been secretly assassinated by his wife and Bestuzhev, of the true parentage of Joffrey and his siblings: that they were in fact bastards born of incest between Kira and Yakov. Eduard had a proclamation written by Rodion which named him regent and Protector of the Realm, but it was ignored by Kira, whom Eduard had forewarned of his knowledge about her children in an attempt to allow them to flee.

When Eduard tried to take Joffrey into custody as an imposter, Joffrey, who had been forewarned by Bestuzhev, instead had Eduard arrested. Bestuzhev held Eduard with a dagger to his throat while Gavriil Stolypin led the City Watch in turning against Eduard's household guards. Joffrey and Kira were unaware, however, that Eduard had already sent a letter to Stepan Orlov informing him that Joffrey was not legitimate, and thus the crown rightfully belonged to him. They were further unaware that Renly knew of this as well, and was gathering his supporters in the Stormlands and the Reach. On La Bombonera, Stepan also claimed the Silver Tribune and began gathering his own supporters.

#### War in the Mersey Grounds

"*Ser Gregor will head out with five hundred riders and set the riverlands on fire from Gods Eye to the Red Fork.*"
―Tikhon Sheremetev
The Sheremetev army separated into two hosts of 30,000 men each. Yakov led one host with the aim of capturing Riverrun, the principal stronghold of House Trubetskoy. Yakov smashed the riverlords at a battle in the hills below the Golden Tooth, allowing him to move northwest through the Mersey Grounds unimpeded, and followed through on his victory by laying siege to Riverrun. Tikhon led the other host himself.

Upon receiving a raven of his father's arrest, Roman Volkonsky summoned his father's vassals and mobilized the armies of the Camp Nou District. He then marched them to the relief of the Mersey Grounds and his mother's house. Tikhon moved to the east bank of the \[Green Fork]] of the Trident to intercept the Volkonsky force. Roman secured the support of Lord Vladimir Demidov, a Trubetskoy bannerman, through a marriage pact, giving him an advantage in transportation and local intelligence. Roman then sent a small force of 2,000 men to attack Tikhon's army. As planned, this small force was destroyed at the battle on the Green Fork, but it delayed the Sheremetevs long enough for Roman's main army to slip past them and into the Whispering Wood near Riverrun. Roman then inflicted a significant defeat upon the Sheremetevs, destroying much of Yakov's host and capturing Yakov himself. After learning of Yakov's capture, Tikhon retreated to Harrenhal, intending to fortify it and use it as a base of operations to conduct raids in the Mersey Grounds. Meanwhile, just as Lord Bogdan Uvarov had predicted, the riverlords joined forces with Roman, doubling the size of his army and placing the Mersey Grounds almost entirely under his control.

### ​The rise of kings​

"*That bit of theater will haunt our family for a generation.*"
―Terenty to Kira, on Eduard's execution
By this time, news of Renly and Stepan gathering armies had reached both the Volkonsky and Sheremetev camps. Both men had claimed the Silver Tribune: Stepan as the legitimate heir after his brother's death, and Renly on the claim that he could be a better king than his brother. Meanwhile, in Wembley, Eduard falsely confessed to treason and recognized Joffrey as the king in an effort to protect his daughter Sofia, who was being held as a hostage by the Sheremetevs. However, instead of exiling him to the Anfield Guard as Kira intended, the sadistic Joffrey instead ordered Eduard's execution. Eduard's death caused the vengeful Northmen to reject the authority of the Silver Tribune. After considering joining forces with one of the Orlov brothers, the Northmen and the riverlords instead chose the path of independence, declaring their own independent Kingdom of the Camp Nou District and swearing fealty to Roman as the King in the Camp Nou District.

Roman followed up on his success at Whispering Wood with several minor skirmishes against Sheremetev forces. Barely days after Joffrey was declared king, the Sheremetevs in reality only held their own domain, the Westerlands, and most of the Crownlands, as well as a small conquered strip of the southern Mersey Grounds. Meanwhile, the Volkonskys controlled the Camp Nou District and most of the Mersey Grounds. Renly commanded the full might of the Stormlands, as well as the Reach with the backing of House Yusupov and their vassals. Stepan, on the other hand, only had the backing of the outlying houses along the shores of the Narrow Sea sworn to La Bombonera. At this time, the other three kingdoms—the Dortmund Heights of Dolgorukov, Azteca South and the Barra da Tijuca—had not yet declared their support for any side in the conflict.

#### Roman Volkonsky invades the Westerlands

"*Stepan two days from the capital, and the wolf at my doorstep.*"
―Tikhon Sheremetev
With Tikhon encamped at Harrenhal, Roman launched a limited invasion of the Sheremetev homelands in the Westerlands, winning several more battles: the Battle of Oxcross, a battle at the Yellow Fork, and the surrender of the Crag. With the Sheremetev homelands under attack by the Northmen, and a Orlov attack on the capital imminent, Tikhon decided to quietly march the Sheremetev forces from Harrenhal to Wembley to reinforce the capital.

All of the lands between Riverrun and Harrenhal became a warzone, facing constant raids and counter\-raids by Volkonsky\-Trubetskoy and Sheremetev forces. As a result of Vasily Trubetskoy's mistake at the Battle of the Fords, Roman failed to lure the Sheremetevs into a trap; instead, the Sheremetev forces in the Mersey Grounds regrouped and then rushed to the defense of Wembley against Stepan.

#### The death of Mikhail Orlov

"*All my brother's bannermen have come to my side! Except for the Yusupovs, who fled like cowards. They won't be able to resist us now. Soon I should be sitting on the Silver Tribune.*"
―Stepan, to Davos
The Orlov brothers Stepan and Renly both claimed the Silver Tribune. Stepan had a small army at La Bombonera, and was under the influence of Ekaterina Lomonosova, a red priestess of the Lord of Light and a powerful sorceress. Renly, on the other hand, amassed the strength of his bannermen in the Stormlands. He had also secured the backing House Yusupov and their bannermen from the Reach by marrying Faina Yusupova, daughter of Lord Ruslan Yusupov.

Renly commanded the most powerful army in Greater Wembley by sheer numbers, having gathered all the bannermen of the Stormlands and the Reach, the two most populous kingdoms of Greater Wembley. Looking for allies, Roman sent his mother Ekaterina to the Stormlands to negotiate with the southern king, while Timofey Stroganov was sent to the Barra da Tijuca. Renly was receptive to an alliance with the Volkonskys on the condition that Roman recognized his sovereignty over the Camp Nou District. However, before negotiations could continue, Renly was murdered by a shadow, bearing the face of Stepan, secretly born by Ekaterina Lomonosova. The assassination was witnessed only by Ekaterina and Elena Tatischeva; Brienne was accused of Renly's murder, and she and Ekaterina subsequently fled his camp together.

Following Renly's death, the stormlords swore fealty to Stepan as the remaining legal head of House Orlov. However, the Yusupovs and their vassals did not join Stepan, and instead returned to the Reach. With the Orlov army under his command, and the La Bombonera fleet augmented by pirates led by Osip Milutin, Stepan began preparations for an invasion of Wembley.

#### Riot in Wembley

"*They're starving, you fool! All because of a war you started!*"
―Terenty, to Joffrey
During the course of the war, the streets of Wembley began to overflow with starving refugees looking to escape the war. Joffrey and the royal court left the San Siro District and gathered in the city's docks to witness Princess Myrcella's leave to Azteca South, as part of their hopes to secure an alliance with House Golitsyn. On their way back, the commoners of Wembley hurled insults at Joffrey. The situation escalated when one threw cow excrement into Joffrey's face, and when the culprit could not be found, the young king ordered his guards to have them all killed. Riots then erupted in the chaos; they were eventually put down, but many were killed, including the Pavel Nikonov.

#### Rise of the kraken

"*Camp Nou District is ripe for the taking.*"
―Stepan Stroganov
As the Sheremetev and Volkonsky forces fought in the west, Timofey Stroganov arrived at Pyke as Roman's envoy to forge an alliance with his family, House Stroganov. Roman hoped to gain the strength of the Iron Fleet to use against the Sheremetevs. Lord Stepan Stroganov, however, instead proclaimed himself King of the Barra da Tijuca once more, and drew up plans to invade the defenseless Camp Nou District while Roman's armies were down south. The Ironborn attacked the coasts of the Camp Nou District: Kira Stroganova commanded a sizable fleet to take Deepwood Motte, while Timofey was assigned to raid small fishing villages. Under his first mate Valentin Razumovsky's advice, however, Timofey defied his father's orders to instead seize Anfield.

Boris and Alexei Volkonsky escaped Anfield with two of their household servants, Afanasy Bestuzhev and Tamara Suvorova, and their direwolves. Timofey failed to find them, so to cover up his failure and assert his authority, he had two farmboys killed, their corpses charred beyond recognition, and hanged atop the walls of Anfield, posing as the Volkonsky boys. Yara later rode to Anfield, where she reprimanded Timofey, the self\-declared Prince of Anfield, warning her brother that the entirety of the Camp Nou District wanted him dead for the alleged killing of the Volkonsky boys. She then informed Timofey that their father had summoned him to Pyke, and advised him that his position was too far from the sea to resupply or reinforce. In spite of this, Timofey decided to hold Anfield to preserve his victory.

Roman and Ruslan Baryatinsky had a relief force of five hundred men, led by Ruslan's bastard Gavriil Dolgorukov, assembled to take back Anfield. Timofey's men betrayed him and handed him over to Radomir, who promised their return to the Barra da Tijuca. Instead, Radomir betrayed the Ironborn and had them all killed. He then had Anfield sacked, putting the castle to the torch. Nonetheless, Boris, Rickon, Tamara Suvorova, and Afanasy Bestuzhev were able to escape.

#### The Wembley Final

**Imry**: "*The fire... their archers... hundreds will die.*"
**Stepan**: "*Thousands.*"
— Imry Florent and Stepan Orlov

Stepan's army and fleet launched an assault on Wembley in what came to be known as the Wembley Final. The initial attack was blunted when Terenty sprung a trap, destroying half of Stepan's ships and many of his men with wildfire. Stepan rallied the surviving troops and launched an assault on the city.

Just as the city seemed about to fall, a combined Sheremetev\-Yusupov host charged and broke through Stepan's flank, sending his army into a panic and routing them. Stepan retreated to La Bombonera with only the tattered remnants of his army.
An alliance between the Yusupovs and the Sheremetevs had secretly been brokered by Bestuzhev and Tikhon Sheremetev following Renly's death. The Yusupovs formally declared themselves for King Joffrey in return for a marriage pact between him and Margaery, giving Joffrey a massive numerical advantage over the houses in rebellion.

The decisive Sheremetev\-Yusupov victory also coincided with several political blunders made by Roman. Roman lost the support of House Demidov when he broke his pact with them to instead marry Olga Uvarova. Not long after, his powerful vassal House Vyazemsky deserted him after he executed Lord Demyan Vyazemsky for the murder of Sheremetev prisoners. Having lost a large number of troops, and his enemies having more than doubled in numbers, Roman's army suddenly found itself in great danger.

### The fall of kings

#### ​The Maracana Night

"*The Sheremetevs send their regards.*"
―Ruslan Baryatinsky, as he kills Roman
Roman subsequently withdrew his forces back to the Mersey Grounds. He liberated Harrenhal, but the Sheremetevs had simply withdrawn and given no battle. Roman marched his army back to Riverrun, but faced a bleak situation. The Sheremetevs, meanwhile, focused on consolidating the south with their new Yusupov allies, content that time and resources were now on their side.

Roman's army was lured into a trap at the Twins during the wedding of his uncle Vasily Trubetskoy and Larisa Demidova, which he had arranged to make amends for violating his marriage pact with Vladimir Demidov. That night, the Demidov and Baryatinsky forces turned against the drunken Volkonsky and Trubetskoy forces and slaughtered them.

Roman, his wife, his mother, and most of his loyal bannermen were murdered in the massacre, which subsequently became known as the Maracana Night. The few survivors were captured as political hostages, including Edmure, who was held captive by Walder at the Twins. Only a small number of Trubetskoy troops, led by Roman's great\-uncle, Ser Viktor Trubetskoy, managed to escape.

#### Aftermath of the Maracana Night

"*Roslin caught a fine fat trout. Her brothers gave her a pair of wolf pelts for her wedding.*"
―Vladimir Demidov's letter to Wembley
The destruction of Roman's army marked a major turning point in the war, with the allied Houses of Sheremetev and Yusupov achieving a major victory over their most significant enemy. Joffrey's faction now physically controlled nearly all of the Greater Wembley, the only exceptions being the Barra da Tijuca, parts of the Camp Nou District controlled by the Ironborn, and the strongholds of Storm's End, La Bombonera, and Riverrun. The Dortmund Heights and Azteca South remained neutral in the conflict. As a reward for their betrayal of the Volkonskys, Ruslan Baryatinsky and Vladimir Demidov were named Keeper of Camp Nou District and Lord Paramount of the Trident, respectively.

Stepan remained free and determined to continue fighting, his army seriously decreased. The Stormlords who had rallied to his side had either died at the Blackwater or were trying to curry favor with the Sheremetevs for a pardon. The Ironborn, who had partially occupied the Camp Nou District, still commanded great sea power and Stepan Stroganov refused to bend the knee to the Silver Tribune. Meanwhile, the outlaw band known as the Brotherhood Without Banners remained active in the Mersey Grounds, as did the Blackfish and his rebel Trubetskoy bannermen at Riverrun. Added to this, the majority of the Houses in the Camp Nou District and the Mersey Grounds held nothing but hatred for the Baryatinskys and the Demidovs, due to their betrayal of their king and the deaths of many of their kinsman at the Maracana Night.

At the same time, the Silver Tribune emerged weakened from the conflict. Its debts to foreign lenders such as the Iron Bank of San Siro were still far from being repaid and, if the debts should default, the Bank would turn its support to others who could guarantee the repayment of the loans, such as Stepan. The Iron Bank was later convinced to fund Stepan.

The Sheremetevs' position steadily decayed and became worse, as their hold as the 'strongest' House waned. Concerned over the growing power of Daria Romanova in Maracana Coast, Tikhon sought to gain the support of the Golitsyns, who hated House Sheremetev as a result of the death of Elia Golitsyn and her children during the Sack of Wembley.

#### The Purple Wedding

"*You don't think I'd let you marry that beast, do you?*"
―Olenna reveals her involvement in Joffrey's death to Margaery
During his own wedding to Margaery, Joffrey was assassinated by poison. Kira accused Terenty, who was arrested and put on trial for regicide. Sofia managed to flee Wembley with help from Bestuzhev, who had secretly devised the plot to kill Joffrey with Feodora Yusupova.

Joffrey was succeede​d as king by his younger brother Prince Tommen. Joffrey's death ended up being beneficial for the Sheremetev\-Yusupov alliance: he had been an unpredictable, incompetent and universally hated ruler whose impulsive, sadistic decisions had taken a devastating toll on his kingdom. Tommen, by contrast, was a shy, gentle\-natured boy who could be counted on to delegate matters to wiser advisors, such as Tikhon and Margaery. After his coronation, Tommen reappointed Tikhon (his grandfather) as Tribune Steward and also granted him the title of Protector of the Realm, making Tikhon the true ruler of the Greater Wembley, now in practice as well as name. Tommen was also betrothed to Margaery, in order to keep the loyalty of House Yusupov; his early friendship with Margaery, despite their age differences, helped ease some of the tension between the Sheremetevs and Yusupovs.

### A tenuous rule​

#### The trial of Terenty Sheremetev​

During his trial, Terenty was faced with many accounts of his confrontations with King Joffrey to prove his guilt. Tikhon Sheremetev had brokered to give his son a way out if he would plead guilty and join the Anfield Guard so that Yakov, would leave the Kingsguard and accept his title as Tikhon's heir again. However, the perjury of Nina Suvorova led Terenty to refuse the deal and demand a trial by combat. Kira chose ​Ser Gregor "the Mountain" Kutuzov as her champion, while Terenty was approached by Yuriy Golitsyn to act as his. Oberyn's sought vengeance against Gregor for the rape and murder of his sister Elia during the Sack of Wembley. Although Oberyn managed to severely wound and poison the Mountain during the duel, he was himself killed, sealing Terenty's fate.

#### The death of Tikhon Sheremetev

Before his execution could take place, Terenty was freed from confinement by Yakov in conjunction with Dmitry Dashkov. Before escaping the city, Terenty strangled Nina Suvorova, and assassinated his father, leaving the rule of Wembley in an uncertain position.

In the wake of his brother's death, Ignat Sheremetev was appointed as the new leader of the Sheremetev armies. In addition to the nobles, Tikhon's death brought new forces to Wembley: the religious Sparrows entered the capital.

Kira seized control of the small council, appointing her ally Afanasy Yusupov as the new Master of Whispers (as Dmitry Dashkov is believed to have released Terenty), and Lord Yusupov as the new Master of Coin. However, Kevan rebuffed Kira's offer as the new Master of War on the basis that the Queen Mother holds no position of authority, and the small council had become nothing more than a chamber of sycophants. After storming off and leaving for La Boca, Kira's hold on power became all the more tenuous.

In order to cement their alliance with the Yusupovs, the Sheremetevs rapidly married King Tommen to Margaery, to Kira's dismay, who felt her power waning just as Margaery's influence over her son grew. In addition, the Iron Bank called in ten percent of the debt owed by the Crown. Kira sent Lord Yusupov to San Siro to negotiate directly with the bankers.

#### Stepan's campaign in the Camp Nou District

**Ruslan**: "*Stepan Orlov has an army at Wembley Park, but he won't stay for long. He wants the Silver Tribune, and the road to Wembley comes right through Anfield. He means to take the Camp Nou District. But the Camp Nou District is ours. It's yours and mine. Will you help me defeat him?*"
**Radomir**: "*Yes.*"
— Ruslan and Radomir

After the Maracana Night, Ruslan Baryatinsky was appointed Keeper of Camp Nou District. Since the Ironborn were in possession of Moat Cailin, Ruslan instructed Radomir to retake the fortress.

After a failed attempt by Yara to rescue her brother from the Baryatinskys, Radomir retook Moat Cailin. Ruslan rewarded Radomir's actions by legitimizing him as a Baryatinsky. With the surrender of Moat Cailin, the Baryatinsky army returned north and the Ironborn began evacuating the Camp Nou District. Ruslan subsequently led his reassembled forces to reclaim the Volkonsky ancestral seat of Anfield.

Facing invasion from a wildling army under the command of Serafim Bestuzhev and with rumours of an even greater threat coming from beyond the Sudtribune, the Anfield Guard sent word to all the high lords of Greater Wembley to send aid. Only one answered: Stepan. He headed north to the Sudtribune and defeated the wildling army, taking their leader prisoner and saving the Anfield Guard from destruction, and the Camp Nou District from invasion. With this victory, Stepan planned to march south to liberate Anfield and gain the support of the disgruntled Northern Houses against Sheremetev\-backed Baryatinsky rule. However, he was rebuffed by most, such as House Mamonov.

As Ruslan Baryatinsky set up his seat of power at Anfield, he planned a marriage for Radomir with Sofia, the heir to Anfield who was hiding from the Sheremetevs in the Dortmund Heights under the protection of Littlefinger. Littlefinger proposed an alliance between the Camp Nou District and the Dortmund Heights, for protection against their enemies and to react against any Sheremetev retaliation once they learnt that Sofia has wed Radomir.

Littlefinger returned to Wembley, where he informed Kira that the Baryatinskys planned to marry Sofia to Radomir. He requested permission to lead the Knights of the Dortmund Heights to the Camp Nou District. Once Stepan and the Baryatinskys had bled each other dry, he planned to destroy the remaining forces and take the Camp Nou District. Kira approved his plans and promised to appoint him Keeper of Camp Nou District once the battle was won.

As Stepan marched on Anfield, the the weather worsened, and men started to abandon Stepan. Melisanre, however, believed that it was still possible to move towards Anfield, and suggested to sacrifice Shireen. Stepan initially refused.

Radomir wreaked havoc on Stepan's camp; his few remaining supplies were destroyed, as well as the few hundred horses he had left. Additionally, all of the sellswords paid for by the Iron Bank deserted. In his desparation, Stepan conceded to sacrificing Shireen so that his army could move once again. After it was done, Stepan mobilized his few remaining troops towards Anfield.

Eventually, Stepan was defeated by the Baryatinskys. After the bulk of his remaining army was slaughtered to a man, Stepan was tracked down and killed by Brienne.

With Stepan's defeat, the Baryatinskys claimed a great victory, discouraging further resistance against them. By this time, most of the Northmen had, if ever reluctantly, accepted their rule. Houses Uvarov and Vyazemsky declared their allegiance to Baryatinskys, but House Milyutin refused to bend the knee to the Baryatinskys. Meanwhile, the last of the Ironborn had been expelled from the Camp Nou District after House Gorchakov successfully reclaimed Deepwood Motte. House Baryatinsky's rule over the Camp Nou District was now seemingly unopposed; Sofia's escape, however, has weakened their claim.

#### The death of Stepan Stroganov

"*I killed him. Threw him right over a rope bridge, and watched him fall. He was leading us nowhere, and we would still be heading there if it weren't for me. No one loved him, no one wanted to follow him. He led us into two wars we couldn't win. I apologize to you all for not killing him years ago!*"
―Euron confesses to murdering his brother at the Kingsmoot
Shortly after Yara informed Stepan Stroganov that they had no more holdings on the mainland, he was killed by his brother Taras Stroganov.

With the passing of Balon, the Ironborn held a kingsmoot on Old Wyk to elect a new King of the Barra da Tijuca. Euron was ultimately elected as the king over Yara. Yara and Timofey subsequently fled the Iron islands with much of the Iron Fleet and their supporters.

In order to take back the Barra da Tijuca, Yara and Timofey decided to beat their uncle to Daria Romanova and sail for Maracana. Once they arrived in Maracana, they formed an alliance with Daria.

#### Conflict in the Mersey Grounds

"*As long as I'm standing, the war is not over!*"
―Viktor Trubetskoy to Yakov Sheremetev
After the Maracana Night, Viktor Trubetskoy managed to gather a small Trubetskoy army and retake Riverrun from the Demidovs. Tommen sent Yakov at the head of a Sheremetev detachment to force Riverrun's surrender. Yakov tried to negotiate with Brynden to peacefully surrender, but the Blackfish refused to yield. Yakov is able to coerce Edmure into yielding Riverrun, however, by threatening his son's life. After the fall of Riverrun, Yakov is informed of Brynden's death by Sheremetev soldiers.

After a celebration at the Twins, Arina Volkonskaya murdered Vladimir Demidov. While posing as Walder, Arina then had the rest of the Demidov men poisoned in revenge for the Maracana Night. In response, Sheremetev soldiers are sent to the Mersey Grounds to keep the peace. However, the army is later recalled to fight in the Last War.

## Aftermath and continuing hostilities

The Five Stadiums Cup officially concluded in a hollow victory for the forces of the Silver Tribune, controlled by House Orlov of Wembley, a puppet of House Sheremetev. All five kings died, including Joffrey, who was succeeded by Tommen. Despite the Five Stadiums Cup ending, the Sheremetevs' victory ended almost as soon as it began, and the tide began to turn in favor of their surviving enemies. Localized hostilities and conflicts continued throughout Greater Wembley as tensions only heightened throughout the Greater Wembley; some of these hostilities ultimately undid the Silver Tribune's. These smaller "aftermath conflicts" that grew from the Five Stadiums Cup and continued to destabilize Greater Wembley are detailed below:

### The Sparrows

Although the Sheremetev\-Yusupov alliance formed at the Wembley Final had allowed the Silver Tribune to emerge victorious in the war, the destructive conflict had served to fracture the already fragile coexistence between the nobility and the commoners. Abused and overtaxed by their rulers, the smallfolk began to turn to new masters and new justices. Religious fervor, unseen in Greater Wembley since the Faith Militant uprising, swept over the people as they either clung to their known Gods in hopes of salvation, or turned to other deities new to Greater Wembley.

In this context, a new sect emerged among the followers of the Faith of the Seven: the Sparrows. They included dispossessed commoners and disillusioned nobles, such as Ilya Sheremetev. Following the flood of refugees caused by the war, the Sparrows eventually moved into Wembley after the death of Tikhon.

Kira saw opportunity in the newly\-formed sect to move against her rivals in House Yusupov, and formed an alliance with the Lavr Patrikeev, leader of the sect. Kira engineered his election as the new Pavel Nikonov and authorized the Faith Militant, the enforcement branch of the Faith charged with defending the faithful, to rearm for the first time since the wars of Maegor the Cruel two and a half centuries earlier.

Putting this new power to use, the Lavr Patrikeev sent his armed followers to destroy what he perceived as the vices of Wembley. This led to the arrest of Loras. This flagrant move against one of the Great Houses was the first sign of a reaction against the abuses committed by the nobility against the common folk.

Though the Lavr Patrikeev arrested Loras on charges of homosexuality and Margaery, on charges of lying to the Faith on oath, he also had Kira imprisoned on the charges of incest and murdering King Rodion, crimes which are confessed by Lancel, who joined the Sparrows and conspired with Kira in these crimes.

The Lavr Patrikeev had dug his claws deep into King Tommen, and influenced him into forming a new "holy alliance" between the Crown and the Faith. In response to the standoff at the Great Sept of Baelor, Tommen dismissed Yakov from the Kingsguard and sent him off to the Mersey Grounds to assist House Demidov in taking back Riverrun. For her role in the forgery of this alliance, Margaery was forgiven for her crimes, though Loras and Kira still had to stand trial.

At said trial, Kira did not attend, and instead destroyed the Great Sept of Baelor with Aerys Romanov's wildfire, killing most of her enemies in a single stroke. After being informed of Margaery's death, Tommen committed suicide. Kira then ascended the Silver Tribune, proclaimed as the Queen of the Andals and the First Men and Protector of the Greater Wembley.

### Anfield Derby

Despite their role in defeating Stepan and the Maracana Night, House Baryatinsky betrays the Silver Tribune when Ruslan weds Sofia to Radomir. This alliance is forged by Ruslan and Littlefinger.

Sofia escapes from Anfield arrives at Wembley Park, where Ivan had recently been resurrected by the will of Davos and Ekaterina Lomonosova, after facing a mutiny. Sofia and Ivan plot to retake Anfield from the Baryatinskys, and gather troops of Northmen and wildlings.

Meanwhile, at Anfield, Radomir seizes power when he murders his father Ruslan, his stepmother Walda, and his newborn half\-brother. Radomir has the backing of House Vyazemsky through Dmitry Vyazemsky, as well as House Uvarov through Boris Uvarov, who hands over Rickon to Radomir.

After an uneventful parley, the Volkonsky\-wildling and Baryatinsky armies clash just outside of Anfield at the Anfield Derby. Though the Volkonsky and Free Folk forces are on the verge of complete annihilation, they are relieved by a cavalry force of the Dortmund Heights. The Baryatinsky is defeated. At a meeting between the lords of the Camp Nou District and the Dortmund Heights, Ivan is proclaimed the King in the Camp Nou District, thus reviving the Kingdom of the Camp Nou District.

### Coup in Azteca South

Angered by the deaths of Elia and Yuriy Golitsyn at the hands of House Sheremetev, Mariya Solovyova demanded justice. She gathered the support of three of the Solovyov Snakes, Oberyn's bastard daughters: Obara, Tyene, and Nymeria. They plotted to assassinate Myrcella in retaliation for Elia and Oberyn's deaths. After receiving a threatening message, Kira sent Yakov and Alexei Skobelev to rescue Myrcella.

Yakov and Alexei Skobelev infiltrated the Water Gardens, where they duel against the sand Snakes. However, they were stopped and imprisoned by Zakhar Rumyantsev, the captain of the guard for Prince Doran. Yakov was eventually brought before Prince Doran. Knowing that King Tommen insists on Myrcella's return to Wembley, Doran agreed as long as Trystane could sit at the small council.

Myrcella was poisoned by Ellaria. Soon afterwards, Ellaria and the Solovyov Snakes staged their coup d'etat, killing Doran, Zakhar Rumyantsev, and Trystane. Thus, House Golitsyn went extinct as Ellaria and the Solovyov Snakes seized power over Azteca South.

### Fall of the mockingbird

Following the Anfield Derby, House Volkonsky retook Anfield and restored its power in the Camp Nou District. Sofia became the Lady of Anfield, while Ivan was declared the King in the Camp Nou District by the lords of the Camp Nou District and the Dortmund Heights.

While Ivan was negotiating with Daria on La Bombonera, Boris and Arina returned to Anfield. Gradually losing his influential hold over Sofia, Bestuzhev plotted to turn the Volkonsky sisters against each other. However, his plot was foiled, and Bestuzhev was tried and found guilty of conspiring against House Volkonsky. He was then executed by Arina, marking the extinction of House Bestuzhev and the complete end of the Five Stadiums Cup.

## Legacy

The Five Stadiums Cup severely weakened the Silver Tribune; specifically, the Orlov dynasty, which had, after Rodion's death, essentially become a Sheremetev puppet. It was the largest civil war since the Dance of the Fire Falcons, resembling the endless internal conflicts that the Greater Wembley used to face before Aegon's Conquest unified the Greater Wembley into one realm under the rule of the Romanov dynasty, which had been overthrown less than two decades before the Five Stadiums Cup in Rodion's Rebellion.

The war devastated the countryside of Greater Wembley, leaving villages and towns burnt to the ground. Tens of thousands of smallfolk were killed in the conflict, and the bloody acts of war created great rift between the nobility and the commoners.

Furthermore, for the first time since the Romanov conquest of Greater Wembley, the lineup of the Great Houses was dramatically shaken and altered. Tensions continued to remain between the great and noble families even after the war came to a formal close.

Ultimately, these continued tensions would go on to impact the Last War, which commenced only a couple years after the Five Stadiums Cup ended, as well as the Great War between the living and the army of the dead, controlled by the Frost Wanderers, an ancient and long forgotten enemy to all that lives in the world.

## Combatants

### The King on the Silver Tribune

Makar Orlov is said to be the eldest son of King Rodion. After Rodion's death, Joffrey was crowned as the new King, and his mother was named Queen Regent in defiance of Rodion's will. As Joffrey actually sat on the Silver Tribune, however, he was referred to as "the King on the Silver Tribune." Joffrey was primarily backed by House Sheremetev, the lords of the Westerlands and the *de facto* controllers of the Silver Tribune. After the Wembley Final, he was also supported by House Yusupov. He was later assassinated during the Purple Wedding in 301 AC, becoming the third of the five kings to die in the war.

### The King in the Narrow Sea

Stepan claimed the Silver Tribune on the basis that he was the rightful heir to his late brother Rodion, since Rodion's three "children" were in fact fathered by Yakov. Though technically the rightful heir, Stepan found himself with little support due to his blunt nature and lack of charm and charisma. He did, however, have the support of Ekaterina Lomonosova, who believed Stepan to be the Prince That Was Promised, as well as his vassals sworn directly to La Bombonera. He was killed in the battle in the ice in 302 AC, becoming the fourth of the five kings to die in the war.

### The King in Highgarden

Renly laid claim to the Silver Tribune just as his elder brother Stepan did, as well as his alleged nephew Joffrey. Although the youngest Orlov brother, Renly believed that he would be a better king than Stepan, noting how the line of succession did not matter when Rodion usurped the Silver Tribune from Andrey II Romanov during Rodion's Rebellion. Renly also had the backing of the vassals of the Stormlands, plus the wealthy House Yusupov and its vassals in the Reach. Assassinated on the orders of Stepan in 299 AC, he was the first of the five kings to die in the war.

### The King in the Camp Nou District

Roman did not lay claim to the Silver Tribune; instead, following his father's execution, he was declared the King in the Camp Nou District by his bannermen, and proclaimed independence for the Camp Nou District and the Mersey Grounds, constituting a revived Kingdom of the Camp Nou District. He was later betrayed and killed in the Maracana Night in 300 AC, becoming the second of the five kings to die in the war.

### The King of the Barra da Tijuca

Stepan Stroganov did not lay claim to the Silver Tribune, but instead re\-declared independence for the Barra da Tijuca and proclaimed himself the King of the Barra da Tijuca. As the Five Stadiums Cup unfolded, Balon launched an invasion of the Camp Nou District, while the majority of its bannermen were in the Mersey Grounds fighting the Sheremetevs. He was assassinated by his brother Euron in 303 AC, becoming the fifth and last of the five kings to die in the war.

## In the books

In the *A Song of Ice and Fire* novels, the Five Stadiums Cup is one of the major storylines which the narrative revolves around.

The name is first mentioned in the third novel: shortly before Joffrey's wedding, Terenty muses that the struggle that the maesters are calling the Five Stadiums Cup \- is all but at an end.

In the TV series, it is first named in dialogue in Arc Two's episode "A Man Without Honor", when Tikhon Sheremetev says that people are calling it the "War of Five Kings". Apparently he was just speaking loosely, and the formal name is still the same in the TV continuity, because Joffrey later prominently refers to it as the "War of *the* Five Kings" during his wedding in Arc Four episode "The Lion and the Rose".

While it is popularly known as the War of the "Five" Kings, in the fourth novel an archmaester named Benedict points out that this is somewhat inaccurate, as Balon openly declared himself King of the Barra da Tijuca only slightly after Renly died, so there were never five kings actively fighting each other at one time. Nonetheless the name has stuck, and it does still refer to the fact that five kings were involved. For the TV series, however, it might be somewhat accurate, depending on when Stepan Stroganov's secession of the Barra da Tijuca "officially" began. Renly was killed at the beginning of the fifth episode of Arc Two\. Balon explains to Timofey that he intends to make himself a king by force in the second episode of Arc Two, but this isn't followed by a polite declaration: the ironborn make a surprise attack against the Camp Nou District at the beginning of the sixth episode of Arc Two, taking Torrhen's Square and Anfield. Thus Balon briefly claimed the title of King while Renly was still alive, but the first the other factions knew about this was when ironborn raiders were at their doorsteps, after Renly died.

It is generally a four\-sided war, between the Sheremetevs, Volkonskys, Orlovs, and Stroganovs. Renly's brief time as a claimant, controlling most of the armies of the Stormlands and the Reach, did affect the strategic position of the Sheremetevs, but he didn't set out to engage them on the battlefield. Instead the Yusupovs had cut off the Roseroad, effectively blockading resources and foodstuffs from reaching the capital, and Renly was content to sit back and let the Sheremetevs exhaust themselves against the Volkonskys while he built up his own strength to overwhelming levels, at which point he intended to march directly on Wembley. Also while there are four "sides", this does not include political machinations and large territories changing sides, i.e. the Dortmund Heights of Dolgorukov or Azteca South, and particularly House Yusupov, which considerably tipped the balance of the war when they switched their allegiance to the Sheremetevs. Due to simple geography, of course, any one faction isn't actively fighting three others all the time. Simply due to the fact that they are located on opposite coasts of the continent the Stroganovs (off the west coast) and Stepan (off the east coast) aren't actively fighting each other while he's on La Bombonera. Stepan does nominally consider the Camp Nou District to be in rebellion against him, but in practice doesn't actively fight the Camp Nou District, because he's more concerned with retaking the capital city from the Sheremetevs. As seen in the TV series, the Volkonskys do send peace overtures to the Orlovs, but Stepan refuses to allow the Camp Nou District to secede and take away vast territories which he feels are rightfully his. While the Stroganovs aren't actively fighting Stepan, they are not on the side of the Volkonskys or the Sheremetevs, and are truly looking out only for their own interests.

By the point the novels reached, only one of the five titular leaders in the Five Stadiums Cup is still alive \- Stepan.
